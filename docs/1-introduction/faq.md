# Common problems and questions

Find your problem below. If it is not here, contact your department office or academy admin.

## Signing in

**APD says my username or password is wrong.**
- Check that you are on **your academy's** address. An account from another academy will not work.
- Check Caps Lock. Passwords are case-sensitive.
- Students: try your **student ID number** instead of your email, or the other way round.
- Still wrong? Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. See [Open APD and sign in](./your-academy-and-address#forgot-your-password).

**I did not get the password reset email.**
Wait a few minutes and check your **spam** or **junk** folder. Make sure you typed the email that is in APD. If nothing arrives, ask your academy admin to reset your password.

**I see "This academy could not be found".**
The address is wrong. Check the spelling, or open **https://apd.et**, click **Academies**, and choose yours.

**My account was deactivated.**
Only your academy admin can restore it. Contact them.

## Students

**I see no activities to report.**
You only see activities where you are an **informant**. Ask your department head to check that:
1. you are enrolled in the course, and
2. you are chosen as an informant for the activity.

**The + button to add a class session is grey.**
You can only report for the **current, open week**. Past and future weeks are closed, and weeks can be locked by the department head. See [Report class sessions](../2-students/report-class-sessions).

**I reported the wrong number of hours.**
You can change or delete your report while the week is still open. After the week closes, ask your department head.

**The qualitative survey is empty.**
The survey only opens at certain moments (checkpoints) in each course. When a checkpoint opens, APD notifies you. See [Qualitative survey](../2-students/qualitative-survey).

**Telegram bot does not show my activities.**
Make sure your Telegram account is connected. See [Telegram](../2-students/telegram-integration).

## Staff

**I can see a department but cannot change anything.**
That is normal. Only the department head of that department can make changes. Other staff can only look.

**I was appointed department head but APD does not show the head menus.**
Send a request from APD. See [Request department head access](../3-instructors/request-department-head-access). Your academy admin or quality lead will approve it.

**I cannot edit a course.**
A course cannot be edited after it has **passed**, while it is **submitted** for approval, or after it is **approved** (archived). See [Submit a course for approval](../4-department-heads/submit-for-approval).

**My menus look different from the pictures.**
Menus depend on your role. The pictures in this guide come from a demo academy.

## Everyone

**How do I switch between light and dark mode?**
Click the **screen icon** in the top bar and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.

**Can I use APD on my phone?**
Yes. APD works in any modern phone browser. On small screens, open the menu with the **☰** button.

**Where do I report a problem with APD itself?**
Staff can use the **Feedback** page in the left menu. See [Things every staff user can do](./common-features).
