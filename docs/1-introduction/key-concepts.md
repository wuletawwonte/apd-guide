# Words you will see in APD

APD uses a few special words. Learn them once, and the rest of the guide will be easy.

## The university structure

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Institution** | Your university, for example "Arba Minch University". |
| **Academy** | A college, institute or school inside the university, for example "Institute of Technology". Each academy has its **own web address** and its own data. |
| **Department** | A department inside an academy, for example "Software Engineering". Most work happens inside a department. |

## Teaching

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Curriculum course** | A course in the department's official catalog. It is the "template" for a course. |
| **Course** | One real run of a course in a semester, with start and end dates. It is created from a curriculum course. |
| **Activity** | One part of a course, for example the lecture, the lab or the tutorial. A course can have several activities. A large class can be split into **groups** (for example Lab group 1 and Lab group 2). |
| **Activity type** | The kind of activity: lecture, lab, tutorial, and so on. |
| **Activity week** | One week of an activity. APD creates the weeks from the activity dates. Students report class sessions for each week. |
| **Class session** | One class meeting. Students report if it was **held** and **how many hours** it lasted. |
| **Expected hours** | How many hours of teaching were planned for a week or a course. |

## People

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Instructor** | The staff member who teaches an activity. |
| **Informant** | A student chosen to report class sessions for an activity. Not every student is an informant. |
| **Enrollment** | Adding a student to a course or an activity. Students only see what they are enrolled in. |

## Measuring quality

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Performance** | How much of the plan really happened, as a percentage. For example, if 4 hours were planned and students reported 3 hours, the performance is 75%. |
| **Reported** (report coverage) | How much of the plan informants reported at all, held **or** not held, as a percentage. If 4 sessions were planned and informants reported 3 of them, Reported is 75%. APD shows it next to performance everywhere, because performance can only count what was reported. Below **80%**, it is shown in orange or red: the performance beside it rests on incomplete reports. |
| **Performance feedback** | The department head's rating of a finished week: *Disastrous*, *Needs improvement*, *Acceptable*, *Good* or *Excellent*. |
| **Qualitative survey** | A short list of statements that students rate, for example "The instructor explains clearly". |
| **Statement** | One question in the qualitative survey. Each statement has answer **options**. |
| **Checkpoint** | A moment in the course when students answer the survey, for example at 25%, 50% and 75% of the course. |
| **Last 6 weeks** (trend) | On the dean, quality office and president dashboards: a small line of weekly delivery (hours delivered against hours planned) over the last six weeks, with last week's change from the week before in points, for example **▲ +8 pts**. |
| **Follow-up** | A request from the dean, the quality office or the president asking a department head to look into their department or one course. It is a conversation: the head and the person who asked reply to each other until the matter is done, and then it is resolved. Each reply notifies the other side. |

## Status words

Courses, activities and weeks show a status based on their dates:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming** | It has not started yet. |
| **Ongoing** / **Active** | It is happening now. |
| **Passed** / **Finished** | It has ended. |
| **Voided** | (weeks only) The week was cancelled, for example because of a holiday. It does not count in performance. |
| **No reports** | (weeks only) The week has ended but no informant reported on it. Its 0% means "unknown", not necessarily "the class did not happen". It still counts as 0% in the activity's score, so check with the informants. |

Courses also move through an **approval** process at the end of the semester:

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | The department is still working on the course. |
| **Submitted** | The department head sent the course to the quality office for approval. |
| **Sent back** / **Returned** | The quality office returned the course with a reason. The department head must fix it and submit again. |
| **Approved / Archived** | The quality office accepted the course. It is now read-only. |
