# Features every staff member uses

This page explains the parts of APD that look the same for all staff: instructors, department heads, quality staff, deans and admins. Learn them once and you can use them everywhere.

::: tip Who is this for?
All staff accounts. Students have a simpler screen. See the [Students guide](../2-students/getting-started).
:::

## The screen at a glance

After you sign in, the screen has three parts:

- **The side menu** on the left. It shows the pages you can open.
- **The top bar** across the top. It has search, the theme button, notifications and your account menu.
- **The main area** in the middle. This is where the page content appears.

![The top bar with the menu button, search box, theme button, notification bell and account button](/screenshots/common/top-bar.png)

| Part of the top bar | What it does |
|---------------------|--------------|
| **☰** (three lines) | Hides or shows the side menu. Useful on small screens. |
| **Search** | Finds courses, activities, departments and people quickly. |
| Screen icon | Changes the colour theme (light, dark or same as your device). |
| Bell | Shows your notifications. A coloured dot means you have unread ones. |
| Your name | Opens your account menu. |

## The side menu

The side menu shows the pages your role allows. Everyone sees the **Extras** group at the bottom.

![The side menu with the department switcher, department pages and the Extras group](/screenshots/common/sidebar.png)

| Menu item | What you find there |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Home** | A summary of the selected department. |
| **Overview** | Performance charts and tables for the department's courses. |
| **Courses** | All courses of the department. |
| **Students** | Students who belong to the department. |
| **Instructors** | Staff who belong to the department. |
| **Curriculum Courses** | The department's list of planned courses (the catalog). |
| **Activity Types** | Kinds of activities, such as Lecture or Laboratory. |
| **Qualitative Analysis** | The student survey (statements, checkpoints and settings). |
| **Feedback** | A form to send a message to the APD team. |
| **Release Notes** | News about changes to APD. A green **new** label means there is news you have not read. |

Some roles see more items, for example **My dashboard** for department heads. Each role's guide explains its extra pages.

## Switch to another department

The box at the top of the side menu shows the department you are looking at. The department pages in the menu (Home, Overview, Courses and so on) always show data for this department.

1. Click the department box at the top of the side menu.
2. Type part of a department name in **Search departments…**, or scroll the list.
3. Click the department you want.

APD opens the home page of that department. It remembers your choice until you change it again.

![The department switcher open, with a search box and a list of departments](/screenshots/common/department-switcher.png)

::: info
A small shield icon next to a department name means you are the head of that department.
:::

## Search for anything

Use search when you know what you want but not where it is.

1. Click **Search** in the top bar. You can also press **Ctrl + K** (Windows) or **⌘ + K** (Mac).
2. Type at least two letters, for example part of a course name or code.
3. Results appear in groups: **Courses**, **Curriculum Courses**, **Activities**, **Departments** and more.
4. Click a result to open it. You can also use the **↑** and **↓** keys and press **Enter**.
5. Press **Esc** to close the search box.

![The search box showing results for "oper" grouped into courses, curriculum courses and activities](/screenshots/common/global-search.png)

You only see results you are allowed to open.

## Change the colour theme

APD has a light theme and a dark theme.

1. Click the screen icon in the top bar.
2. Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Same as device**.

**Same as device** follows the setting of your computer or phone. APD remembers your choice on this device.

![The theme menu with Light, Dark and Same as device](/screenshots/common/theme-menu.png)

![The department home page in the dark theme](/screenshots/common/dark-theme.png)

## Read your notifications

APD sends you a notification when something needs your attention. Examples: a request waiting for your decision, a course that was approved or sent back, or a reply to your feedback.

1. Click the bell in the top bar. A coloured dot on the bell means you have unread notifications.
2. Read the latest notifications in the list. New ones have a **New** label.
3. Click a notification to read it. APD marks it as read. Click **Open related page** to go to the page it is about.
4. Click **Mark all read** to clear the dot.
5. Click **View all** to see every notification on one page.

![The notifications list open under the bell, with Mark all read and View all](/screenshots/common/notifications-menu.png)

![The Notifications page](/screenshots/common/notifications-page.png)

::: tip
If you connect Telegram, you also get your notifications there. See [Connect Telegram](#connect-telegram) below.
:::

## Announcements

Your academy admin or quality office can post announcements. An announcement appears as a blue box at the top of your pages.

![A blue announcement box at the top of a page, with a close (×) button](/screenshots/common/announcement-banner.png)

- Read the title and the message.
- Click **×** on the right to close it. It will not appear again for you.

## Your account menu

Click your name in the top right corner to open the account menu.

![The account menu with My Profile, My Activities, Change Password, Integrations, User guide and Logout](/screenshots/common/account-menu.png)

| Menu item | What it does |
|-----------|--------------|
| **My Profile** | Shows your details, roles and department. |

## View and edit your profile

1. Click your name in the top right corner, then **My Profile**.
2. Your profile shows your name, email, your roles (for example **Regular user**, which means instructor), your department, and the date you joined.

![The My profile page with the Edit profile, Change password and My activities buttons, and Account details](/screenshots/common/profile.png)

To change your details:

1. On your profile, click **Edit profile**.
2. To add a photo, click the round picture or **Upload file**, then choose a PNG, JPG or WebP image.
3. Change your **First name**, **Last name** or **Email** if needed.
4. Choose your **Primary department** from the list.
5. Click **Save changes**. Click **Discard changes** to leave without saving.

![The Edit profile form with Profile photo, Personal information and Department sections](/screenshots/common/edit-profile.png)

::: warning
Your email is also your sign-in name. If you change it, use the new email the next time you sign in.
:::

## Change your password

1. Click your name in the top right corner, then **Change Password**.
2. Type your **Current password**.
3. Type a **New password**. It must have at least 6 characters.
4. Type the new password again in **Confirm new password**.
5. Click **Update password**.

![The Change password form with Current password, New password and Confirm new password](/screenshots/common/change-password.png)

::: tip
Use a password that you do not use on other websites. Never share your password, even with colleagues.
:::

## Connect Telegram

When you connect Telegram, APD sends your notifications to the APD Telegram bot. Some roles can also ask the bot for quick summaries.

1. Make sure Telegram is installed on your phone or computer.
2. Click your name in the top right corner, then **Integrations**.
3. On the **Telegram** card, click **Connect Telegram**.
4. Telegram opens the APD bot. Tap **Start**.
5. The bot tells you that your account is linked. Back in APD, the card now shows **Connected**.

![The Integrations page with the Telegram card and the Connect Telegram button](/screenshots/common/integrations.png)

In the bot, type **/help** to see what you can do. The commands depend on your role:

| Your role | Bot commands |
|-----------|--------------|
| Instructor (regular user) | **/help**, **/disconnect** |
| Department head | **/pendingfeedback** (weeks waiting for your feedback), **/summary** (department snapshot), **/help**, **/disconnect** |
| Education quality lead | **/approvals** (courses waiting for approval), **/summary** (academy overview), **/help**, **/disconnect** |
| Dean | **/summary** (academy overview, also **/overview**), **/help**, **/disconnect** |
| Academy admin | **/summary** (academy snapshot), **/help**, **/disconnect** |

**If you have more than one role**, you get the commands of every role. When two roles use the same command, the bot keeps it for one role and gives the other a different name:

- A dean, quality lead or academy admin who is also a **department head** gets the department snapshot as **/department**. **/summary** shows the academy view.
- An academy admin who is also a **dean** gets the department overview as **/overview**.

Type **/help** at any time to see your own list.

Presidents do not have **Integrations** in their account menu, so they cannot connect Telegram.

To stop using Telegram, click **Disconnect** on the Telegram card in APD, or type **/disconnect** in the bot.

::: info Problems connecting?
- The link from **Connect Telegram** works for **10 minutes** and only once. If the bot says "This link has expired or already been used", go back to **Integrations** and click **Connect Telegram** again.
- If the bot says the account "is already linked", disconnect the old link first, then connect again.
- If you see "Telegram integration isn't configured for this deployment", your university has not set up the bot yet. Ask your academy admin.
:::

## Send feedback to the APD team

Use the **Feedback** page to report a problem, ask a question or suggest an idea.

1. In the side menu, click **Feedback**.
2. Under **What is this about?**, choose **General feedback**, **Bug report** or **Feature request**.
3. Write your **Message**. For a problem, say what you clicked and what went wrong. You can attach a file, such as a screenshot.
4. Click **Send feedback**.

![The Feedback form with three subject choices, a message box and the Send feedback button](/screenshots/common/feedback-form.png)

APD adds your name and email so the team can reply. When the team resolves your feedback, you get a notification.

## Read the release notes

The **Release Notes** page tells you what is new in APD.

1. In the side menu, click **Release Notes**.
2. Read the newest changes at the top. Each note shows a version number and a release date.

![The Release Notes page with a list of versions and what changed](/screenshots/common/release-notes.png)

## The page footer

At the bottom of every staff page you find three small icons:

- **Book**: opens this user guide.
- **Open book**: opens the release notes ("What's new").
- **Paper plane**: opens APD support on Telegram.

## Sign out

1. Click your name in the top right corner.
2. Click **Logout**.

Always sign out when you use a shared or public computer.
