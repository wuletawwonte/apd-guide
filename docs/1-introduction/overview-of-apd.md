# What is APD?

**APD** (Academic Performance Dashboard) is a website that helps a university check the **quality of teaching**.
It answers simple questions like:

- Did the planned classes really happen this week?
- How many hours were taught compared to the plan?
- What do students think about their courses?

Everyone uses APD from a web browser, on a computer or a phone. Students can also send reports through **Telegram**.

![The APD public website](/screenshots/common/public-home.png)

---

## How APD works, in four steps

1. **The department plans the teaching.** The department head adds courses, activities (lectures, labs, tutorials) and weekly plans.
2. **Students report what happened.** Each week, selected students (called **informants**) tell APD if a class was held and for how long.
3. **Staff review the results.** The department head rates each week. Deans, the quality office and the president see charts and reports.
4. **The quality office closes the course.** At the end, the department head submits the course. The quality office approves it, and the course is archived.

Students also answer a short **qualitative survey** a few times during each course. This tells the university how students feel about the teaching.
