# Browse your department

As an instructor you can look at your department's courses, performance, students, instructors and catalog. These pages are **read-only** for you: you can open and read them, but only the department head can add or change things.

All these pages are in the side menu, under the department box. They always show the department that is selected in that box. See [Switch to another department](../1-introduction/common-features#switch-to-another-department).

## Overview: department performance

The **Overview** page shows how all courses of the department are going.

1. In the side menu, click **Overview**.
2. The **Courses** tab opens. It lists every course with two scores:
   - **Qual.** — the average score from the student survey (qualitative).
   - **Reported** — how much of the planned teaching informants reported at all. Orange or red means the next score rests on incomplete reports.
   - **Quantitative** — how much of the planned teaching took place so far.
3. Use the buttons to filter the list: **All**, **Ongoing**, **Upcoming** or **Done**.
4. Choose **Session** or **Hour** to change how teaching is measured.
5. Type in the search box to find a course by name or code.
6. Click a course row to open its full analytics.

![The Overview page, Courses tab, with filters and a performance bar for each course](/screenshots/instructors/department-overview.png)

::: tip Download the list
Click the download button (arrow icon, next to the search box) to save the list as a CSV file. You can open it in Excel.
:::

Click the **Overview** tab (next to **Courses**) to see charts:

- **Performance metrics by course** — hourly, session-based and qualitative scores for each course.
- **Course status** — how many courses are high (above 80%), medium (50–79%) or low (below 50%).

![The Overview tab with a bar chart of performance by course and a Course status doughnut chart](/screenshots/instructors/department-overview-charts.png)

For one course's analytics, see [See how the whole course is going](./my-activities#see-how-the-whole-course-is-going).

## Courses

1. In the side menu, click **Courses**.
2. The list shows each course with its code, the number of activities (small grey number) and its **Status**:
   - **Ongoing** — teaching is happening now.
   - **Upcoming** — it starts later.
   - **Passed** — it has ended.
3. Type in **Search courses…** to find a course. Use **Status** (top right) to show only some statuses.
4. Click a course name, or **Open**, to see the course.

![The Courses list with names, codes, status labels and Open links](/screenshots/instructors/courses-list.png)

A course page has two tabs:

- **Activities** — the parts of the course, such as Lecture and Laboratory, with their type and expected hours. Click **Weeks** on a row to see that activity's weeks.
- **Course details** — dates, year, semester, status and other information about the course.

![A course page, Activities tab, listing the course's activities](/screenshots/instructors/course-activities.png)

![A course page, Course details tab, with the course's dates, year, semester and status](/screenshots/instructors/course-details.png)

## Students

1. In the side menu, click **Students**.
2. The list shows each student's name, email and **ID number**.
3. Use the page numbers at the bottom to see more students.
4. Click **Open** to see one student.

![The Students list with names, emails, ID numbers and Open links](/screenshots/instructors/students-list.png)

The student page shows the student ID, the department, the last sign-in time, the total number of sign-ins and the date they joined.

![A student's page with student ID, last sign-in, total logins and member since](/screenshots/instructors/student-detail.png)

::: warning Protect student data
Student information is private. Use it only for your academic work.
:::

## Instructors

1. In the side menu, click **Instructors**.
2. The list shows all staff of the department, including the department head.
3. Click **Open** to see a person's page. It shows their department, their role and the activities they teach (**Assigned activities**).

![The Instructors list with names, emails and Open links](/screenshots/instructors/instructors-list.png)

## Curriculum Courses

The **curriculum** is the department's catalog of planned courses. Each real course that runs in a semester is made from a curriculum course.

1. In the side menu, click **Curriculum Courses**.
2. The list shows each course's **Name**, **Code**, **Year & term** and total **Hours**. The small grey number after the name is the number of planned activities.
3. Use **Rows per page** at the bottom to show more rows.

![The Curriculum Courses list with name, code, year and term, and hours](/screenshots/instructors/curriculum-courses.png)

## Activity Types

**Activity types** are the kinds of teaching the department uses, for example **Lecture** (`LEC`), **Laboratory** (`LAB`) and **Tutorial** (`TUT`).

1. In the side menu, click **Activity Types**.
2. The list shows each type's **Name** and **Code**.

![The Activity types list with Laboratory, Lecture and Tutorial](/screenshots/instructors/activity-types.png)

## Qualitative analysis: the student survey

Students fill in a short survey about each course at set points in the semester. The **Qualitative analysis** page shows this survey.

1. In the side menu (under **Workspace**), click **Qualitative analysis**.
2. The **Statements** tab shows each question students answer, and the answer choices (for example from **Strongly disagree** to **Strongly agree**). A red star (**\***) means the question must be answered.
3. The **Settings** tab shows the survey's title and description.

The **Checkpoints** tab (when in the course the survey opens) is for the quality office. Instructors cannot open it.

![The Qualitative analysis page with the Statements, Checkpoints and Settings tabs and the list of survey statements](/screenshots/instructors/qualitative-analysis.png)

The survey is managed by the education quality lead. To see the survey results for a course, open the course's analytics and click the **Survey** tab.

## Common questions

**Can I add a student or change a course?** No. Ask your department head.

**Why do I see edit buttons missing that my head has?** Instructors have read-only access. Buttons like **Add course**, **Edit** or **Delete** appear only for the department head.

**Can I see other departments?** Yes. Choose another department in the department box at the top of the side menu.
