# Request department head access

Department heads are appointed by the university, outside APD. When you are appointed head of a department, you ask APD for the matching **head access**. An **academy admin** or the **quality office** (education quality lead) checks your appointment and grants it.

With head access you can manage your department's courses, activities, weeks, students and instructors, and give weekly feedback. See the [Department heads guide](../4-department-heads/overview).

::: tip Who can ask?
Instructors (**Regular user**) and existing department heads (for another department). Staff with another role, such as quality lead, dean, admin or president, cannot ask.
:::

## How to send a request

1. Click your name in the top right corner, then **My Profile**.
2. In **Account details**, find the **Head access** row. Click **Request head access**.

   ![The My profile page with the Request head access link highlighted in the Head access row](/screenshots/instructors/profile-request-head-access.png)

3. A window opens. In **Department**, choose the department you head. You can type to search the list.
4. In **Appointment reference or note** (optional), write something the reviewer can check. For example: "Appointed by the dean, letter 2026/114".
5. Click **Send request**.

   ![The Request department head access window with a Department list, an Appointment reference box and the Send request button](/screenshots/instructors/request-head-access-modal.png)

APD shows "Your request for head access to … was sent." The academy admins and quality leads get a notification.

::: warning If the department already has a head
The list shows the current head in brackets, for example "Software Engineering (head access: Meron Alemu)". If your request is granted, **that person loses head access** to the department. Make sure the appointment is official before you ask.
:::

## Follow your request

While your request waits, your profile shows it with a yellow **Pending** label and the text "waiting for review".

![The Head access row on the profile showing a Pending request for Software Engineering with a Withdraw button](/screenshots/instructors/head-access-pending.png)

To see all your requests, click **View my requests** in the request window. The **My head access requests** page lists each request with the date, **Status** and **Decision**.

![The My head access requests page with a pending request and a Withdraw button](/screenshots/instructors/my-head-access-requests.png)

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Waiting for an admin or the quality office. |
| **Granted** | You now have head access. |
| **Declined** | The request was not accepted. The reason is shown. |
| **Withdrawn** | You cancelled the request. |

::: info
If nobody at your academy decides within 3 days, the APD platform team may decide instead.
:::

## Withdraw a request

You can cancel a request while it is still **Pending**.

1. Open **My Profile** (or **My head access requests**).
2. Click **Withdraw** next to the request.
3. Confirm by clicking **OK** in the message "Withdraw your request for head access to …?".

The status changes to **Withdrawn**.

![The My head access requests page showing the request as Withdrawn and the message "Request withdrawn."](/screenshots/instructors/head-access-withdrawn.png)

## When your request is granted

- You get a notification: "You now have head access to …".
- Your profile shows the department in the **Head access** row with a green **Granted** label.
- The next time you open APD, your home page is **My dashboard**, the department head dashboard.
- A shield icon appears next to the department name in the department box.

Click **Dismiss** next to the decision to hide it from your profile. It stays in your request history.

Now read the [Department heads guide](../4-department-heads/overview).

## When your request is declined

You get a notification with the reason. The common reasons are:

| Reason | What to do |
|--------|------------|
| "You aren't the head of this department on record." | Check your appointment with the dean or the academy admin. |
| "Wrong department. Request the one you head." | Send a new request for the right department. |
| "Your appointment hasn't taken effect yet. Request again once it does." | Wait until your appointment starts, then ask again. |

The reviewer may add more details after the reason.

## Common questions

**I do not see "Request head access".** Your role does not allow it (for example, you are a quality lead or dean), or there are no departments you can request. Ask your academy admin.

**Can I ask for two departments?** Yes. Send one request for each department. You can have only one pending request per department.

**Can the admin make me head without a request?** Yes. The academy admin can also set the head of a department directly.
