# My activities and feedback

An **activity** is one part of a course that you teach, for example the **Lecture** or a **Laboratory** group. Each activity is split into **weeks**. Every week:

1. Students who report for the activity (called **informants**) say whether each class session took place and how long it was.
2. APD compares what happened with what was planned and shows a **performance** percentage.
3. After the week ends, your department head rates the week. This is the **feedback**.

This page shows you how to find your activities and read their performance and feedback.

## See the activities you teach

1. Click your name in the top right corner.
2. Click **My Activities**. (You can also open **My Profile** and click **My activities**.)

![The My activities page with a table of assigned activities: code, type, course and expected hours](/screenshots/instructors/my-activities.png)

The table shows every activity where you are the instructor:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Code** | The activity code, for example `SE-SWE-3101-LEC`. Click it to open the activity. |
| **Type** | The kind of activity, for example Lecture or Laboratory. |
| **Course** | The course the activity belongs to. Click it to open the course. |
| **Expected hours** | The total teaching hours planned for the activity. |

The coloured dot before each code shows the activity status:

| Dot | Status |
|-----|--------|
| Green | **Active** — teaching is happening now. |
| Blue | **Upcoming** — it has not started yet. |
| Red | **Passed** — it has ended. |

::: tip Missing an activity?
Only your department head can make you the instructor of an activity. If an activity is missing from this list, ask your department head.
:::

## Check weekly performance and feedback

1. On **My Activities**, click the activity **Code**.
2. The **Weeks** tab opens. It lists every week of the activity.

![The Weeks tab of an activity with planned and actual hours, performance bars and the Feedback column highlighted](/screenshots/instructors/activity-weeks-feedback.png)

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Week** | The week number and its dates. |
| **Planned sessions** | How many class sessions were planned for the week. |
| **Planned hours** | How many hours were planned. |
| **Actual hours** (or **Actual sessions**) | What the students reported. |
| **Performance** | Actual compared with planned, as a percentage. |
| **Feedback** | The department head's rating for the week. |
| **Actions** | For past weeks, "Closed to late entries" means students can no longer report for that week. |

Use **Measure by** (top right) to switch between **Hour** and **Session**. Hour compares hours taught. Session compares the number of sessions held.

### What the colours mean

| Colour of the performance bar | Performance |
|-------------------------------|-------------|
| Green | 80% or more |
| Yellow | 50% to 79% |
| Red | Below 50% |

### What the feedback labels mean

Your department head chooses one of five ratings:

| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Excellent** | The week went as planned or better. |
| **Good** | The week went well, with small gaps. |
| **Acceptable** | The week was acceptable, but could be better. |
| **Needs improvement** | Important parts of the week were missed. |
| **Disastrous** | Most of the week did not take place. |

- **Pending** means the week has ended but the head has not rated it yet.
- A dash (**—**) means the week has not ended yet, or it was removed from the plan.
- The head can add a comment. **Point your mouse at the rating** to read the comment.

::: info
You see the **Feedback** column only on activities where **you** are the instructor.
:::

## See activity details

On an activity page, click the **Activity details** tab. It shows the activity code, type, course, year and semester, start and end dates, remaining days, status, instructor, duration in weeks, sessions per week and expected total hours.

![The Activity details tab with code, type, course, dates, status, instructor and planned hours](/screenshots/instructors/activity-details.png)

## See how the whole course is going

Your activity is part of a course. To see all activities of the course together:

1. In the side menu, click **Overview**.
2. Click the course name in the list.
3. The **Performance** tab shows each activity of the course with its **Actual**, **Expected** and **Total** hours, last week's result and the result **So far**.

![Course analytics, Performance tab, with a breakdown of activities and their progress bars](/screenshots/instructors/course-analytics.png)

Two more tabs give more detail:

- **Weekly** — a line chart of each activity's performance, week by week.
- **Survey** — results of the student survey for this course: number of submissions, average score and number of statements. Click **Qualitative details** for the answers to each statement.

![Course analytics, Weekly tab, with a line chart of weekly performance by activity](/screenshots/instructors/course-analytics-weekly.png)

![Course analytics, Survey tab, with survey submissions, average qualitative score and survey statements](/screenshots/instructors/course-analytics-survey.png)

## Common questions

**Why is my performance low when I taught every class?** Performance comes from what the students report. If informants forget to report, the week shows less than was taught. Remind your informants to report every week, or tell your department head.

**Can I change the planned hours or give feedback myself?** No. Planned sessions, hours and feedback are managed by the department head.

**Why does a week show "Closed to late entries"?** The week has ended and the department head stopped late reports for it.
