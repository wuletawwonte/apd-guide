# Getting started as an instructor

Welcome! This part of the guide is for **instructors** and other teaching staff. In APD your role is called **Regular user**.

APD helps your department follow how teaching is going. Students report each week whether class sessions took place. Your department head reviews each week and gives feedback. As an instructor, you can follow the activities you teach, see how they are going, and read the feedback.

## What you can do in APD

- See your department's courses, activities, students and instructors.
- Follow the weekly performance of the activities you teach.
- Read the feedback your department head gives on your weeks.
- Look at performance charts for your department.
- Ask for department head access, if you have been appointed head.
- Send feedback to the APD team and read what is new.

Most pages are **read-only** for instructors. Your department head creates courses, activities and weeks. If something is wrong, contact your department head.

## Your first steps

1. **Get an account.** Your academy admin or department head usually creates it for you. You can also [create your own staff account](./setup-account) if your academy allows it.
2. **Sign in** on your academy's address. See [Sign in to APD](./login-instructions).
3. **Look around your home page.** See [Your home page](./home-page).
4. **Check your activities.** See [My activities and feedback](./my-activities).
5. **Learn the shared tools** like search, notifications and your profile. See [Features every staff member uses](../1-introduction/common-features).
