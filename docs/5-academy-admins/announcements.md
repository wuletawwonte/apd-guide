# Announcements

Announcements are short messages that appear at the top of staff pages. Use them for deadlines, meetings, system maintenance or policy reminders. You can show a message to everyone in your academy, or only to some departments, roles or people.

::: tip Who can do this
Academy admins.
:::

## Where staff see announcements

An active announcement shows as a blue box at the top of these pages:

- the admin **Dashboard**,
- a department's **Home** page,
- the department head's **My dashboard**.

Each person can close the box with the **×** button. It then stays hidden for that person only.

![An announcement shown at the top of a staff page](/screenshots/admins/dashboard-banner.png)

::: info Students do not see announcements
Announcements are for staff only. To reach students, use your usual channels (for example, the department office or Telegram groups).
:::

## See all announcements

1. In the left menu, click **Announcements**.
2. The list shows each announcement's **Title**, **Scope**, **Audience**, whether it is **Active**, and when it **Starts at** and **Ends at**.
3. Use **Search by title** to find one.

![The list of announcements](/screenshots/admins/announcements-list.png)

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Scope** | **Academy** — made for your academy. **Global** — sent by the APD support team to every academy. You cannot change global announcements. |
| **Audience** | **Everyone**, or the groups it targets, such as **1 department** or **1 role**. |
| **Active** | **Active** announcements can show. **Inactive** ones never show, whatever their dates. |

In the **Actions** column, the **eye** opens the announcement, the **pencil** edits it and the red **bin** deletes it.

## Create an announcement

1. Click **Announcements** in the left menu.
2. Click **New Announcement**.
3. **Announcement Details**:
   - **Title** — a short headline, for example *Semester reporting deadline*.
   - **Message** — the full text. Use the toolbar for **bold**, *italic*, links, lists and quotes.
4. **Scheduling**:
   - **Start Date & Time** — when it starts to show. Leave it empty to show it straight away.
   - **End Date & Time** — when it stops showing. Leave it empty to show it until you turn it off.
5. **Audience** — choose who sees it (see [Choose the audience](#choose-the-audience)).
6. **Status** — leave **Active** turned on.
7. Click **Save**.

![The New Announcement form filled in](/screenshots/admins/announcement-new-filled.png)

APD opens the announcement's page so you can check it.

::: tip Always set an end date
Old messages make people stop reading announcements. An end date removes the message for you when it is no longer true.
:::

## Choose the audience

In **Audience**, pick one option:

- **Everyone in this academy** — every staff member, whatever their department or role.
- **Only specific people** — narrow it down. Tick any mix of:
  - **Departments** — everyone whose department is ticked.
  - **Roles** — everyone who has a ticked role (**Academy admin**, **Academy dean**, **Education quality lead**, **Department head**, **Regular user**).
  - **Named people** — pick people one by one. Type in **Search people…** to find them.

A person sees the announcement if they match **any** of your choices. The grey line at the bottom tells you in plain words who will see it, for example *Anyone matching 1 department or 1 role will see this announcement.*

![The audience options with one department and one role ticked](/screenshots/admins/announcement-audience.png)

::: warning
- Every staff member has the **Regular user** role. Ticking it reaches the whole academy.
- If you choose **Only specific people** but tick nothing, the announcement still goes to everyone. APD shows a yellow warning when this happens.
:::

## View an announcement

Click the title (or the **eye** icon). The page shows the message, scope, status, dates and targets. **Views** tells you how many people have closed (dismissed) it.

![An announcement's details page](/screenshots/admins/announcement-show.png)

## Edit an announcement

1. Open the announcement and click **Edit**. (Or click the **pencil** in the list.)
2. Change the text, dates, audience or status.
3. Click **Save**.

## Stop or delete an announcement

- **To stop it for now:** edit it and turn off **Active**, or set the **End Date & Time** to now. You can turn it back on later.
- **To remove it for good:** open it and click **Delete** (or click the red **bin** in the list), then click **OK**.

## Common questions

**I saved an announcement but I don't see it.**
Check that it is **Active**, that the start time has passed, and that the end time has not. Check that you are in the audience. If you closed it before, it stays hidden for you.

**Can I see who read it?**
APD counts how many people dismissed it (**Views**). It does not show who read it without dismissing.
