# Head access requests

Department heads are appointed by the university, outside APD. When a newly appointed head needs APD access, they can send a **head access request**. You check that the appointment is real, then **grant** or **decline** the request.

::: tip Who can decide
Academy admins and education quality leads. Either of you can decide; the first decision counts.
:::

## How you learn about a request

When a staff member sends a request, APD tells you in three places:

- **Notifications** — a new item in the bell menu, top right: *… requested head access to …*.
- **Dashboard** — a line at the top: *1 head access request waiting for review.* with a **Review** button.
- **Departments** — the department has a label such as **1 head access request** in the list.

![A head access request in the notifications menu](/screenshots/admins/notifications-menu.png)

## See all waiting requests

1. On your **Dashboard**, click **Review** next to the *head access requests waiting for review* message.
2. The **Head access requests** page lists all waiting requests, oldest first.

![The Head access requests page](/screenshots/admins/head-access-reviews.png)

Each row shows:

| Column | What it tells you |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Requested by** | The person's name, email, phone (if they added one), when their account was made and when they last signed in. |
| **Department** | The department they say they head, and **Head access now** — who has head access today (or *nobody*). |
| **Appointment reference** | What they gave you to check, for example an appointment letter number. A yellow label warns you when **other requests** exist for the same department. |
| **Requested on** | The date of the request. **Waiting …** shows when it is getting old. |

You can also decide from the department page: open the department under **Departments**. Waiting requests appear at the bottom, under **Head access requests**.

![Head access requests on a department page](/screenshots/admins/department-head-requests.png)

## Check before you decide

Before you grant access, confirm the appointment outside APD. For example:

- Check the appointment letter or the reference number they gave.
- Ask the dean or the office that made the appointment.
- Call or email the person using the contact details in the row.

## Grant access

1. Find the request.
2. Click **Grant access**.
3. If APD asks you to confirm, read the message and click **Grant access**.

APD asks you to confirm when granting will take access away from someone:

- the person who has head access today loses it, and
- any **other waiting requests** for the same department are declined.

The person becomes the department head straight away and gets a notification. The department page now shows them under **Department head**.

![The department page after the request was granted](/screenshots/admins/department-head-granted.png)

## Decline a request

1. Find the request.
2. Click **Decline**.
3. Pick a reason (you must pick one). The person will see it, so choose the one that tells them what to do next:
   - **You aren't the head of this department on record.**
   - **Wrong department. Request the one you head.**
   - **Your appointment hasn't taken effect yet. Request again once it does.**
   - **Another reason (write it below)** — type your own reason. The box is required with this choice.
4. Add more details in the box if needed.
5. Click **Decline request**.

![The Decline window with reasons to choose from](/screenshots/admins/decline-dialog.png)

The person gets a notification with your reason, and their request shows as **Declined**. They can send a new request later.

## If nobody decides

Please decide requests quickly. If a request waits more than **3 days**, or if your academy has no admin or quality lead, the APD support team may decide it instead.

## Other ways to change a head

You do not have to wait for a request. You can attach or detach a head yourself on the department page. See [Departments](./departments#attach-a-department-head).

## Common questions

**Two people asked for the same department. What do I do?**
Only one person can head a department. Find out which appointment is current. Grant that request; the other one is declined automatically.

**Who can send a request?**
Staff members who are regular users or department heads, with no other oversight role. They send it from their profile page.
