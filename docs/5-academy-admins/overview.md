# Academy admins — overview

As an **academy admin**, you look after APD for your whole academy (your college, institute or school). You set up departments, create accounts for staff and students, choose who heads each department, send announcements and keep the academy settings correct.

You only see and change **your own academy**. Other academies in your university have their own admins.

::: tip Who is this for?
Staff who have the **Academy admin** role. If you do not see **Administration** at the top of the left menu, you do not have this role. Ask another academy admin or the APD support team.
:::

## What you can do

| Area | What you do there | Page |
|------|-------------------|------|
| **Dashboard** | See the health of your academy and what needs attention. | This page |
| **Departments** | Add and edit departments. Attach or detach the department head. | [Departments](./departments) |
| **Users** | Add staff accounts, give roles, reset passwords, deactivate accounts. | [Staff users](./staff-users) |
| **Students** | Add student accounts, reset passwords, deactivate accounts. | [Students](./students) |
| **Announcements** | Show messages to all staff or to chosen groups. | [Announcements](./announcements) |
| **Settings** | Change academy-wide values such as the date format. | [Academy settings](./academy-settings) |
| Head access requests | Confirm or decline staff who say they head a department. | [Head access requests](./head-access-requests) |

## Open the admin area

1. Open your academy address, for example `https://yourcollege.apd.et`.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. APD opens your admin **Dashboard**.

The left menu shows your admin pages under **Administration**. Under **Extras** you also find **Qualitative Analysis**, **Feedback** and **Release Notes**, which every staff member has.

![The admin menu on the left side of the screen](/screenshots/admins/sidebar.png)

::: info Admins do not get the department menu
Department heads, deans and quality leads see a department switcher and department pages (courses, students, instructors) in the left menu. Admins manage the academy from the **Administration** pages instead. If you also need to run courses for a department, the academy must make you that department's head.
:::

## Understand the dashboard

The dashboard is the first page you see after you sign in. Open it at any time with **Dashboard** in the left menu.

![The academy admin dashboard](/screenshots/admins/dashboard-full.png)

### Totals at the top

Four cards count what is in your academy:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Departments** | How many departments exist, and how many are inactive. |
| **Staff** | How many staff accounts exist, and how many signed in during the last 30 days. |
| **Students** | How many student accounts exist, and how many signed in during the last 30 days. |
| **Courses** | How many courses exist, and how many are running now. |

### Needs attention

This row lists problems you should fix. When everything is fine, you see **All clear**. When a tile has a number above zero, it is highlighted and lists the people or departments. Click a name to open it, or click the link at the bottom of the tile.

| Tile | Why it matters | What to do |
|------|----------------|------------|
| **Departments with no head** | Nobody can run courses, weeks or feedback in that department until it has a head. | Open the department and [attach a head](./departments#attach-a-department-head). |
| **Staff with no department** | They can sign in, but a department head cannot give them an activity to teach. | [Edit the user](./staff-users#edit-a-staff-user) and choose a department. |
| **Students with no department** | They cannot be enrolled in a course. | Ask the student to join their department from **My Profile**. See [Students](./students#a-student-has-no-department). |
| **Never signed in** | Accounts created more than a week ago that nobody has used. | Check the email address is correct. [Send a password reset](./staff-users#reset-a-password) if needed. |

::: tip
Staff with oversight roles (dean, quality lead, admin, president) often have no department. It is normal to see them under **Staff with no department**.
:::

### Announcements, newest staff and newest students

- **Announcements** lists the messages staff can see right now, with how many are scheduled for later and how many have expired. Click **Manage** to open the [Announcements](./announcements) page.
- **Newest staff** and **Newest students** list the accounts added most recently. **Not signed in yet** means the person has never used the account. Click **View all** to see the full list.

### Head access requests

When a staff member asks for department head access, a line appears at the top of the dashboard. Click **Review** to decide. See [Head access requests](./head-access-requests).

![A notice at the top of the dashboard about waiting head access requests](/screenshots/admins/dashboard-pending-requests.png)

## What admins cannot do

- **Change another academy.** Each academy has its own address, data and admins.
- **Change a president's account.** President accounts are managed by the APD support team. You see **Managed by the super admin** on their page.
- **Create a new academy or university.** Only the APD support team can do this.
- **Delete a department.** You can make it **inactive** instead. See [Departments](./departments).
- **Tick the Department head role on a user.** A person becomes a department head only when you attach them to a department.

## Checklist before each semester

- [ ] Every active department has a **head** (the **Departments with no head** tile shows 0).
- [ ] New staff and students have accounts, with the correct **department**.
- [ ] Staff who left the academy are **deactivated**.
- [ ] Old announcements are turned off or deleted, and new ones are scheduled.
- [ ] Any **head access requests** are decided.
