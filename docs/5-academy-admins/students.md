# Students

The **Students** page lists every student account in your academy, across all departments. Here you add students, fix their details, reset passwords and close accounts.

::: tip Who can do this
Academy admins. Department heads can also add and manage students in their own department. Students can also sign up themselves on the sign-in page.
:::

## See all students

1. In the left menu, click **Students**.
2. The list shows each student's **Name** and email, **Department**, and whether the account **Is active**.
3. To find a student, type a name, email or ID number in **Search by name, email, or ID number** and press **Enter**.

![The list of students](/screenshots/admins/students-list.png)

In the **Action** column, the **pencil** edits the account, the **eye** opens it, the red **bin** deactivates it, and the green **arrow** restores a deactivated account.

## Add a student

1. Click **Students** in the left menu.
2. Click **New Student**.
3. **Department** — click the list and pick the student's department. Type part of the name to search. This field is required.

   ![Searching for a department in the New Student form](/screenshots/admins/student-new-department-picker.png)

4. **student data** — enter the **Id number** (the student's official ID, for example `SWE/0101/16`), **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**.
5. Click **Save**.

![The New Student form filled in](/screenshots/admins/student-new.png)

What happens next:

1. APD creates the account with a **temporary password** and emails it to the student.
2. The student signs in with their **email or ID number** and the temporary password.
3. APD then asks them to choose a new password.

::: warning Choose the department before you click Save
If APD shows an error (for example, **Email has already been taken**), the form comes back and the **Department** box is empty again. Pick the department again before you save.
:::

## Open a student

Click a student's name. The page shows their status, department, and sign-in details such as **Last sign-in** and **Total logins**.

![A student's page with the Account menu open](/screenshots/admins/student-account-menu.png)

## Edit a student

1. Open the student and click **Edit**. (Or click the **pencil** in the list.)
2. Correct the **Id number**, **First name**, **Last name** or **Email**.
3. Click **Save**.

![The Edit student form](/screenshots/admins/student-edit.png)

::: info
The department is chosen only when the student is added. The edit form does not change it. Students can join or change departments themselves from **My Profile** in the student portal.
:::

## Reset a student's password

1. Open the student.
2. Click **Account**, then **Reset password**.
3. Click **OK** to confirm.

APD emails the student a link to choose a new password. Students can also use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.

## Deactivate a student

Deactivate a student who has left, graduated, or should no longer report in APD. Their past reports and survey answers stay.

1. Open the student.
2. Click **Account**, then **Deactivate**.
3. Click **OK** to confirm.

You can also click the red **bin** icon in the list. The student can no longer sign in, and shows as **Deactivated User** with a red cross.

![A deactivated student in the list, with the green restore arrow](/screenshots/admins/students-deactivated.png)

## Restore a student

1. Open the deactivated student.
2. Click **Account**, then **Restore**. (Or click the green **arrow** in the list.)
3. Click **OK** to confirm.

The student can sign in again with their old password.

::: info
You cannot permanently delete a student account. Deactivate it instead.
:::

## A student has no department

The admin dashboard shows **Students with no department** when a student belongs to no department. They cannot be enrolled in courses until they join one. This usually happens when a student removes their only department from their profile.

Ask the student to:

1. Sign in and open **My Profile** (click their name, top right).
2. Add their department in the departments section.

## Common questions

**A student says APD shows no activities.**
Adding a student does not enrol them in courses or activities. Their department head enrols them. Ask the head to check the student's course and activity enrolments.

**Two students share an email address.**
Every account needs its own email. Ask one student for a different email address.

**Can a student also be staff?**
They need two separate accounts with two different emails: a student account and a staff account.
