# Audit log

The **Audit log** shows who changed what in your academy, and when. Use it to check who created or deactivated an account, gave a role, set a temporary password, changed a department or a setting, or decided a course or a head access request.

::: tip Who can do this
Academy admins.
:::

## What the audit log records

APD adds an entry when someone:

- Creates, edits, deactivates or restores a staff or student account.
- Gives or removes roles.
- Sends a password reset link, sets a temporary password, or resends a confirmation email.
- Creates or edits a department, or appoints or removes a department head.
- Changes an academy setting.
- Approves or returns a course, or decides a head access request.

Entries **cannot be edited or deleted**, by you or anyone else.

## How to open the audit log

1. In the left menu, under **Administration**, click **Audit log**.
2. The newest entries are at the top.

![The Audit log page with a few entries](/screenshots/admins/audit-log.png)

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **When** | How long ago it happened. Point at it to see the exact date and time. |
| **Who** | The person who made the change. |
| **Action** | What they did, for example **User created**, **Student temporary password set** or **Course approved**. |
| **Subject** | The account, department, course or request it was about. Click it to open that record. |
| **Details** | What changed, for example the fields that were edited or the roles added or removed, and the IP address it came from. |

## How to filter the list

1. Open **All actions** and choose one kind of action, for example **User temporary password set**.
2. Open **Everyone** and choose one person, to see only their changes.
3. The list updates at once. To show everything again, click **Clear** next to the filters.

If nothing matches, APD shows **No audit entries match these filters.**

## Common questions

**What does "signed in as them" mean?**
Someone from the APD support team signed in as that person to help them. The entry shows the person's name and then the support person's name, for example *Abebe Kebede (Sara, signed in as them)*.

**Can I remove a wrong entry?**
No. The audit log is a permanent record. Nobody can edit or delete entries.

**Does it show students' class reports or weekly feedback?**
No. The audit log is about accounts, roles, passwords, departments, settings and decisions, not about everyday teaching records.
