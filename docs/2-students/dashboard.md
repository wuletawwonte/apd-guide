# Your home page

Your home page opens after you sign in. It shows what you need to do right now. You can come back to it any time by clicking **APD** at the top left.

![The student home page](/screenshots/students/dashboard.png)

## The top menu

![The student top menu](/screenshots/students/navbar.png)

| Item | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **APD** logo | Takes you back to your home page. |
| **My activities** | Lists every activity you report on. See [Report class sessions](./report-class-sessions). |
| **Qualitative survey** | Lists your courses and open feedback windows. See [Qualitative survey](./qualitative-survey). |
| Screen icon | Changes the colour theme: **Light**, **Dark** or **Same as device**. |
| Bell | Your notifications. A red dot means you have unread messages. See [Notifications](./notifications). |
| Your name | Opens your account menu: **My Profile**, **Change Password**, **Integrations**, **User guide** and **Logout**. |

On a phone, **My activities** and **Qualitative survey** are behind the **☰** button at the top left.

![The phone menu opened with the ☰ button](/screenshots/students/mobile-menu.png)

## The three number cards

| Card | What the number means |
|------|------------------------|
| **Active weeks** | Weeks that are open now and still accept your reports. |
| **Course enrollments** | How many courses your department enrolled you in. |
| **Class sessions left** | How many class session reports you still need to send for the open weeks. Try to bring this to **0** each week. |

## Active activity weeks

This list shows each activity that has an open week. For each row you see:

- The **course** name and the activity type code (for example **LEC** for lecture or **LAB** for laboratory).
- The status (**Active** means the week is happening now).
- The week dates.
- On the right, the **week name** and your progress, for example **1/2**. This means you reported 1 of the 2 sessions planned for that week. Filled dots are reported sessions; grey dots are still missing.

Click a row to open that week and report. See [Report class sessions](./report-class-sessions).

Click **View all activities** to see all your activities, including finished and upcoming ones.

::: info "No active weeks right now"
You see this message when no week is open for you. This is normal between weeks, before a course starts, or if you are not an informant for any activity yet.
:::
