# Create your student account

Many academies create student accounts for you. If your department did not, you can sign up yourself on your academy's APD address.

::: tip Check first
If your department already created your account, do **not** sign up again. Go to [Sign in](./login-instructions) and use your ID number. If you do not know your password, use **Forgot password?**.
:::

## Before you start

You need:

- Your academy's APD address, for example `https://yourcollege.apd.et`. Your department gives you this link.
- Your **student ID number**.
- An **email address** you can open. APD sends password reset messages there.

## How to sign up

1. Open your academy's APD address in a browser.
2. On the sign-in page, click **Sign up** at the bottom.
3. Under **Register as**, choose **Student**. The **ID number** field appears.
4. Type your **ID number** exactly as it is on your student ID card.
5. Type your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
6. Open the **Department** list and choose your department. You can type to search.
7. Type your **Email**.
8. Type a **Password** (at least 6 characters). Type it again in **Password confirmation**.
9. Tick **I agree with terms and conditions**. You can click the link to read them.
10. Click **Signup**.

![The sign-up form with Student selected](/screenshots/students/sign-up-form.png)

When the form is correct, APD signs you in and opens your home page.

## If sign-up fails

APD shows a message above the form. Common reasons:

| Message is about… | What to do |
|-------------------|------------|
| **Register as** | Choose **Student** before you click **Signup**. |
| **ID number** has already been taken | An account with your ID already exists. Go to sign in, or ask your department. |
| **Email** has already been taken | Someone already uses this email in APD. Sign in, or use **Forgot password?**. |
| Password is too short / does not match | Use at least 6 characters, and type the same password twice. |
| Terms and conditions | Tick the box before you click **Signup**. |

## After you sign up

You may not see any activities yet. Your department must enroll you in courses and choose you as an informant first. When they do, you get a notification and the activities appear on your home page.
