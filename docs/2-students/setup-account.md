# Create your student account

Many academies create student accounts for you. If your department did not, you can sign up yourself on your academy's APD address.

::: tip Check first
If your department already created your account, do **not** sign up again. Go to [Sign in](./login-instructions) and use your ID number. If you do not know your password, use **Forgot password?**.
:::

## Before you start

You need:

- Your academy's APD address, for example `https://yourcollege.apd.et`. Your department gives you this link.
- Your **student ID number**.
- An **email address** you can open. APD sends a confirmation link there when you sign up, and password reset messages later. The address must be complete, for example `abebe@gmail.com`, not `abebe@gmail`.

## How to sign up

1. Open your academy's APD address in a browser.
2. On the sign-in page, click **Sign up** at the bottom. If there is no **Sign up** link, your academy does not allow students to sign up themselves: ask your department office to create your account.
3. Under **Register as**, choose **Student**. The **ID number** field appears.
4. Type your **ID number** exactly as it is on your student ID card.
5. Type your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
6. Open the **Department** list and choose your department. You can type to search.
7. Type your **Email**.
8. Type a **Password** (at least 6 characters). Type it again in **Password confirmation**.
9. Tick **I agree with terms and conditions**. You can click the link to read them.
10. Click **Signup**.

![The sign-up form with Student selected](/screenshots/students/sign-up-form.png)

When the form is correct, APD takes you back to the sign-in page with the message **A message with a confirmation link has been sent to your email address.** You cannot sign in yet. First confirm your email.

## How to confirm your email

1. Open your email. Look for the message **Confirm your APD account**. If it is not in your inbox, check your spam or junk folder.
2. Click **Confirm my account**.
3. APD opens the **Confirm your account** page. Type the **Password** you chose when you signed up.
4. Click **Confirm and sign in**.

![The Confirm your account page](/screenshots/students/confirm-account.png)

APD signs you in, opens your home page and shows **Your email address has been successfully confirmed.**

::: warning Confirm within 3 days
The link works for **3 days**. If you do not confirm within **7 days**, APD deletes the sign-up and you need to sign up again.
:::

::: tip Forgot the password you chose?
On the **Confirm your account** page, click **Reset it**. Setting a new password also confirms your account. Signing in with **Sign in with Microsoft** (if your academy uses it) confirms your account too.
:::

## If you did not get the confirmation email

1. Go to the sign-in page and click **Didn't get the confirmation email?**.
2. Type the **Email** you signed up with.
3. Click **Resend confirmation email**.

APD sends a new link if that email has an account waiting for confirmation. Check your spam folder too. You can ask for a new link up to 3 times in 15 minutes.

If the link says it has **expired** or is **no longer valid**, ask for a new one the same way.

## If sign-up fails

APD shows a message above the form. Common reasons:

| Message is about… | What to do |
|-------------------|------------|
| **Register as** | Choose **Student** before you click **Signup**. |
| **ID number** has already been taken | An account with your ID already exists. Go to sign in, or ask your department. |
| **Email** has already been taken | Someone already uses this email in APD. If it is you, sign in, use **Forgot password?**, or click **resend the confirmation email** if you have not confirmed it yet. |
| **Email** is invalid | Check the address. It needs a full domain, for example `@gmail.com`. |
| Password is too short / does not match | Use at least 6 characters, and type the same password twice. |
| Terms and conditions | Tick the box before you click **Signup**. |

## After you confirm your email

You may not see any activities yet. Your department must enroll you in courses and choose you as an informant first. When they do, you get a notification and the activities appear on your home page.
