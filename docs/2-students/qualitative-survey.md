# Answer the qualitative survey (course feedback)

The qualitative survey asks you to rate how each of your courses is being taught. You answer a short set of questions a few times during each course. Your answers help your academy's quality office improve teaching.

::: tip Who can do this
Every student enrolled in a course. You do not need to be an informant.
:::

## When can I give feedback?

You cannot answer the survey at any time. It opens only in special weeks called **feedback windows** (or **milestones**).

- Each milestone is a point in the course, for example **25%**, **50%** and **75%** of the way through.
- The window is open for the **whole week** (Monday to Sunday) that contains that point.
- You can answer **once per milestone, per course**.
- When the course has ended, all its windows are closed.

Your quality office decides the milestones. APD sends you a notification when a window opens.

## See your courses and open windows

1. Click **Qualitative survey** in the top menu.
2. You see a card for each course you are enrolled in, with the course level, semester, dates and a timeline bar.
3. Look at the bottom of each card:

| What you see | What it means |
|--------------|---------------|
| A blue **Feedback · 50% milestone** button | A window is open **now**. The dates under the button show until when. |
| **Next window** | No window is open now. The box tells you the next milestone and its week. |
| **This course has ended; feedback windows are closed.** | You can no longer give feedback for this course. |
| **There is no open feedback window for this course right now.** | You already answered all milestones so far, or there are no more milestones. |

![The Qualitative survey page with open windows and a next window](/screenshots/students/qualitative-survey-courses.png)

## How to give feedback

1. On the **Qualitative survey** page, click the blue **Feedback · …% milestone** button on the course card.
2. The survey opens. At the top you see the course name and the dates of this window.
3. Read each question. Click **one** answer for each question (for example from **Strongly disagree** to **Strongly agree**).
4. Questions with a red **\*** are **required**. You must answer them.
5. When you have answered, click **Submit feedback** at the bottom.

![The course feedback form](/screenshots/students/survey-form.png)

The selected answer is highlighted:

![A question with one answer selected](/screenshots/students/survey-answered-question.png)

After you submit, APD takes you back to your courses and shows **Survey was successfully submitted.** The button for that milestone is gone, and the card shows the next window instead.

![The courses page after the feedback was submitted](/screenshots/students/survey-submitted.png)

::: warning You cannot change your answers
Once you click **Submit feedback**, your answers are saved and cannot be edited. Read your answers before you submit.
:::

## If you see "Please answer all required questions."

You missed at least one question marked with a red **\***. Scroll through the form, answer the missing questions, and click **Submit feedback** again.

![The error message when a required question is missing](/screenshots/students/survey-required-error.png)

## Common questions

**Are my answers private?** Your answers are used to measure course quality. Answer honestly — the goal is better teaching.

**I left the page before submitting.** Nothing was saved. Go back to **Qualitative survey** and open the course again while the window is still open.

**The button is gone but I did not submit.** The window has closed (it is only open for one week). Wait for the next milestone.

**I see "Course feedback is not available."** Your academy has not turned on the survey yet.
