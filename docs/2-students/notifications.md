# Notifications

APD sends you short messages about your courses and activities. They appear under the **bell** at the top right.

## What APD tells you about

- **You were chosen as an informant** for an activity. The activity now appears in **My activities**.
- **A feedback window is open** for one of your courses. See [Qualitative survey](./qualitative-survey).
- **Weekly reminders** when you still have class sessions to report: on **Saturday** ("the week ends tomorrow") and, if some are still missing, a last call on **Sunday afternoon** ("reporting closes tonight"). Reports for a week lock at midnight on Sunday.

APD may also send the same message by email, and by Telegram if you [connected Telegram](./telegram-integration).

## How to read your notifications

1. Look at the **bell** at the top right. A red dot means you have unread notifications.
2. Click the bell. A list of your newest notifications opens. New ones have a **New** label.
3. Click a notification to read it in full.

![The notifications list under the bell](/screenshots/students/notifications-dropdown.png)

4. On the full message page, click **Open related page** to go straight to the activity or course.

![One notification opened in full](/screenshots/students/notification-detail.png)

Opening a notification marks it as read.

## See all notifications

1. Click the bell.
2. Click **View all**.

The **Notifications** page shows every message. Unread ones have an **Unread** label, and the **Unread** box at the top shows how many are left.

![The Notifications page](/screenshots/students/notifications-page.png)

## Mark everything as read

Click **Mark all as read** (in the bell list or on the Notifications page). The red dot disappears.
