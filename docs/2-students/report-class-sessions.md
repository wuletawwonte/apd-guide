# Report class sessions

When you are an **informant** for an activity, you tell APD what happened in class each week: was the class held, and for how many hours? Your department uses these reports to check that teaching goes as planned.

::: tip Who can do this
Students chosen as informants for an activity. Your department head chooses informants.
:::

## Words you will see

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Activity** | One part of a course, for example the **Lecture** or the **Laboratory**. One course can have several activities. |
| **Activity week** | One week of an activity, for example **Week 9**. Each week has a number of planned sessions. |
| **Class session** | One class meeting. You report each one, even when the class did **not** happen. |
| **Session progress** | How many sessions you reported out of the sessions planned for that week, for example **1/2**. |

## Open your activities

1. Click **My activities** in the top menu.
2. The buttons at the top are **activity types** (for example **Lecture** and **Laboratory**). Click one to see those activities.
3. Each card shows the course, its dates, a progress bar for the weeks that have passed, and its status.
4. Click a card (or **Open weeks**) to open its weeks.

![The My activities page with Lecture and Laboratory buttons](/screenshots/students/my-activities.png)

The coloured border and label show the activity status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** (green) | The activity is running now. |
| **Upcoming** (blue) | The activity has not started yet. You cannot report yet. |
| **Passed** (red) | The activity has ended. |

## Choose a week

At the top of the activity page there is a row of **week buttons** (Week 1, Week 2, …). The current week is selected for you. A green tick on a week button means you can still add reports to that week.

On small screens, swipe the row left or right to see more weeks.

![An activity page with the week buttons and the current week](/screenshots/students/activity-weeks.png)

## The fastest way: your home page

For the current week you don't need to open the activity at all. Your [home page](./dashboard) lists every open week with **Held**, **Not held** and **All … held** buttons and an hours box that starts at the planned length. One click saves the report, and an **Undo** button appears for a few seconds in case you clicked the wrong one. See [Report a session from your home page](./dashboard#report-a-session-from-your-home-page).

Use the activity page (below) when you want to see the reports you already sent, report a past week that your department head reopened, or edit a report.

## How to report a class session on the activity page

1. Open the current week.
2. Click the blue **+** button at the top right of the week card.

   ![The + button on the week card](/screenshots/students/add-session-button.png)

3. The **Log class session** window opens. Under **Class held?**:
   - Choose **Yes** if the class took place. **Session length in hours** already shows the planned length of one session (the line under the box says, for example, **Planned: 2 hours per session**). Change it if the class was shorter or longer.
   - Choose **No** if the class did not take place. The hours box disappears: a class that did not happen has no hours.

   ![The Log class session window with the planned hours filled in](/screenshots/students/log-class-session-modal.png)

4. Click **Save report**. There is no separate confirmation step: if you made a mistake, edit or delete the report (see below).

Your report now appears under **Logged sessions**, and the **Session progress** dots update.

![The week card after two sessions were reported](/screenshots/students/after-submit.png)

In the list of logged sessions:

- A **green tick** means you reported that the class was held.
- A **red cross** means you reported that the class was not held.
- The date shows when you sent the report.

::: info Hours are whole numbers
Session length is a whole number of hours, from 1 to 12.
:::

When all planned sessions of a week are reported, the **+** button turns grey. If you have reported everything in all your open weeks, APD shows a short "Nice work" message.

::: tip Report every planned session
If a week has 2 planned sessions, send 2 reports — one for each class. Send a report even when the class did not happen; choose **No** for it.
:::

## How to fix a mistake

You can edit or delete your own report **until the end of the week in which you sent it** (Sunday night). After that, the report is locked.

**To edit a report:**

1. Find the report under **Logged sessions**.
2. Click the **pencil** icon.
3. Change **Class held?** or the hours.
4. Click **Save changes**.

![The Edit session report window](/screenshots/students/edit-session-modal.png)

**To delete a report:**

1. Click the red **bin** icon next to the report.
2. Confirm that you want to delete it. This cannot be undone.

If you move your mouse over the pencil or bin icon, APD shows the last day you can still change the report. If the icons are gone, the report is locked. Ask your department head if it must be corrected.

## Why can't I click the + button?

The **+** button is grey when you cannot add a report to that week:

| Week label | Why the + button is grey |
|------------|--------------------------|
| **Upcoming** | The week has not started yet. Come back when it starts. |
| **Passed** | The week is over, so reporting is closed. Your department head can reopen a past week for a short time. When they do, the **+** button works again until the date they set. |
| **Voided** (name crossed out) | The department cancelled this week, for example because of a holiday. Nothing needs to be reported. |
| **Active**, all dots filled | You already reported all planned sessions for this week. |

![A past week with the + button turned grey](/screenshots/students/past-week.png)

## Report from your phone

The same steps work in a phone browser. On the home page the report buttons wrap under each week; on the activity page the windows open from the bottom of the screen.

![Logging a class session on a phone](/screenshots/students/mobile-log-session.png)

You can also report through Telegram. See [Report through Telegram](./telegram-integration).

## Common questions

**I don't see any activities.** You are not an informant for any activity yet. Ask your department head. You get a notification when you are chosen.

**I reported the wrong number of hours last week.** If the report is locked, ask your department head to correct it.

**The week shows 0/2 but I already reported on Telegram.** Refresh the page. Reports from Telegram and from the website are the same.

**I pressed a button twice. Did I report two sessions?** Only if the week had room for two. APD never records more reports than the sessions planned for a week, and you can use **Undo** or the bin icon to remove an extra one.

**Why can't I report for an activity I'm not assigned to?** Only the activity's informants can report its sessions. APD checks this every time, on the website and in Telegram.
