# Your profile, departments and password

Your profile holds your name, contact details and the departments you belong to. You can also change your password and the colour theme.

## Open your profile

1. Click your name or picture at the top right.
2. Click **My Profile**.

![Your profile page](/screenshots/students/profile.png)

The profile shows your picture (or your initials), your **ID number**, **Email** and **Phone**, and the date you joined.

## How to edit your profile

1. On your profile, click **Edit profile**.
2. To add a photo, click **Choose file** next to **Profile photo** and pick a PNG or JPG picture.
3. Change your **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** or **Phone number** if needed.
4. Click **Save changes**. To leave without saving, click **Cancel**.

![The Edit profile form](/screenshots/students/edit-profile.png)

::: info About your ID number and email
You cannot change your **ID number** yourself. Ask your department if it is wrong. Keep your **email** correct — password reset links are sent there.
:::

## Change your email

When you change your **Email** and click **Save changes**, APD does not switch to the new email at once. It shows **Profile updated. We sent a confirmation link to** your new email.

1. Open the new email inbox and find the message **Confirm your new APD email address**.
2. Click **Confirm my new email**.

APD shows **Your email address has been successfully confirmed.** From now on, sign in with the new email. Until you confirm, you still sign in with your old email, and the **Edit profile** form shows that it is waiting for you. The link works for 3 days.

::: tip Phone number
The phone number is optional. If you add one, use the international format, for example `+251911000000`. Each phone number can belong to only one student.
:::

## Your departments

Under **My departments** you see every department you belong to. One of them is your **primary** department (it has a green tick).

![The My departments list with the Add department button](/screenshots/students/my-departments.png)

### Join another department

Some students belong to more than one department, for example for a minor.

1. Click **Add department**.
2. Choose the **Department** from the list. Only departments you have not joined are listed.
3. Click **Join department**.

![The Add department window](/screenshots/students/join-department-modal.png)

### Change your primary department

In the **Primary** column, click the empty circle next to the department that should be your primary one. The green tick moves to it.

### Leave a department

1. Click the red **bin** icon at the end of the row.
2. Click **Leave department** to confirm.

You cannot leave your primary department (its bin icon is grey). Make another department primary first.

## How to change your password

1. Click your name at the top right, then **Change Password**. (Or click **Password** on your profile.)
2. Type your **Current password**.
3. Type your **New password**, then type it again in **Confirm new password**.
4. Click **Save new password**.

You stay signed in after the change.

![The Change password page](/screenshots/students/change-password.png)

If you forgot your current password, sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. See [Sign in and sign out](./login-instructions#forgot-your-password).

## Change the colour theme

Click the **screen** icon at the top right and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Same as device**.

![The theme menu](/screenshots/students/theme-menu.png)
