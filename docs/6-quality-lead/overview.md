# Education quality lead — overview

This section is for the **education quality lead** (the quality office) of your academy (your college, institute or school). It shows you where to start and what you can do in APD.

::: tip Who is this for
Staff who have the **Education quality lead** role. Your academy admin gives you this role.
:::

## What you do in APD

As the quality lead, you look after teaching quality across **all departments** of your academy. In APD you:

| Task | Where you do it | Read more |
|------|-----------------|-----------|
| Watch how every department is doing | **Quality office dashboard** | [This page](#the-quality-office-dashboard) |
| Review courses that department heads have closed | **Course approvals** | [Approve or return courses](./course-approvals) |
| Set up the student feedback survey | **Qualitative analysis** | [Survey settings and checkpoints](./qualitative-survey-setup) |
| Write the survey questions and answers | **Qualitative analysis** → **Statements** | [Statements and answer options](./statements-and-options) |
| Look inside any department and manage its curriculum | **Department** menu | [View departments and curriculum](./viewing-departments) |
| Confirm who heads each department | **Head access requests** | [Head access requests](./head-access-requests) |
| Ask a department head to look into a problem | **Follow-ups** → **New follow-up** | [Ask for a follow-up](#ask-a-department-head-to-follow-up) |

## Sign in

1. Open your academy address, for example `https://yourcollege.apd.et`.
2. Enter your email and password, then click **Login**.
3. APD opens the **Quality office dashboard**.

## Your menu

The menu on the left has two parts.

![The quality lead's left menu with the Department item and the Quality office items](/screenshots/quality-lead/sidebar.png)

- **Navigation → Department** opens the pages of one department. See [View departments and curriculum](./viewing-departments).
- **Quality office** holds your own tools:
  - **Quality office dashboard** — the summary of all departments.
  - **Course approvals** — courses waiting for your decision. A number shows how many are waiting.
  - **Head access requests** — staff asking for department head access. A number shows how many are waiting.
  - **Follow-ups** — what you asked department heads to look into, and their replies.
  - **Qualitative analysis** — the student feedback survey.

At the top right you find the search box, the help menu (**?**, with **What's new**, the user guide and **Send feedback**), your **notifications** (the bell) and your account menu (**My Profile**, **Change Password**, the **Theme** switch, **Logout**). See [Features every staff member uses](/1-introduction/common-features).

## The quality office dashboard

The dashboard shows one row for each department in your academy. When courses are waiting for you, a note at the top says so, for example **"1 course waiting for your review."** Click **Review** to open **Course approvals**.

![Quality office dashboard with one row per department](/screenshots/quality-lead/dashboard.png)

| Column | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Department** | The department name. Click it to open the department's analytics. On smaller screens the head's name shows under it. |
| **Head** | The person who has department head access. A dash (—) means nobody. Shown on wide screens only. |
| **Courses** | All courses of the department. Hidden on phones. |
| **Ongoing** | Courses that are running now. Hidden on phones. |
| **Avg hourly %** | On average, how much of the planned teaching hours really happened, over ongoing courses. A course with no hours planned yet (it has just started) is left out, so it does not pull the average down. |
| **Last 6 weeks** | A small line of the department's weekly delivery over the last six weeks, with last week's change from the week before (for example **▲ +8 pts** in green or **▼ −12 pts** in red). Point at a week for its numbers. Shown on wider screens only. |
| **Avg reported %** | How much of the planned teaching informants reported at all (held or not), averaged over ongoing courses. Orange or red (below 80%) means the hourly figure rests on incomplete reports. |
| **Avg qualitative %** | The average score students gave in the feedback survey. |
| **Course bands** | How many courses are **High** (80% or more), **Med** (50–79%) and **Low** (below 50%) by hourly delivery. This counts every course with a score so far, finished ones too, while the averages cover ongoing courses only. Shown on very wide screens only. |
| **Open** | The house icon opens the department's **Home** page. The flag icon (**Follow up**) asks the head to look into something. The chart icon opens its **Analytics**. |

::: tip Read the numbers together
A low **Avg hourly %** means classes are being missed, unless **Avg reported %** is low too, in which case informants are not reporting. A low **Avg qualitative %** means students are not happy with how the course is taught. Open the department to find the courses behind the number.
:::

## Ask a department head to follow up

When a department or a course needs attention, ask its head to look into it. The head sees the request at the top of their dashboard and resolves it with a reply.

1. In the left menu, click **Follow-ups**, then click **New follow-up**.
2. Under **Which department should look into it?**, choose the department and click **Continue**.

   You can also start from the dashboard: click the **flag** icon (**Follow up**) in the department's row. To ask about one course, open the course's analytics and click **Follow up** at the top. Both skip this step.
3. Under **Course**, leave **The whole department**, or choose a course.
4. In **What should the head look into?**, say what you noticed and what you want to know.
5. Click **Send follow-up**.

The **Follow-ups** page lists every follow-up in the academy, including those sent by the dean and the president. The **Open** tab shows the ones still waiting for a reply, **Resolved** shows the answered ones with the head's reply, and **All** shows both. The **Follow-ups** button at the top right of the dashboard opens the same page; its badge shows how many are open. For more detail, see [the dean's guide](../7-deans/dean-dashboard#ask-a-department-head-to-follow-up): it works the same way.

## Download the dashboard as a file

You can save the table as a CSV, Excel or PDF file.

1. Open the **Quality office dashboard**.
2. Click **Export** at the top right of the table. A menu opens.
3. Choose a format:
   - **CSV**: plain text. Opens in Excel, LibreOffice or Google Sheets.
   - **Excel workbook** (`.xlsx`): one sheet, ready to sort and filter.
   - **PDF**: ready to print or share.
4. Your browser saves a file named like `quality-office-dashboard-2026-09-27.pdf`.

## Notes you may see on the dashboard

When courses wait for your review, a note at the top of the dashboard says **"1 course waiting for your review."** Click **Review** to open **Course approvals**.

When a staff member asks for department head access, another note says **"1 head access request waiting for review."** Click **Review** to decide. See [Head access requests](./head-access-requests). You can also open both from the left menu.

You also get a notification (the bell, an email, and a Telegram message if you connected Telegram) when:

- a department head submits a course for approval,
- someone asks for department head access, and
- a department head resolves one of your follow-ups.

## Use Telegram

Connect your account to the APD Telegram bot to get these notifications on your phone. See [Connect Telegram](/1-introduction/common-features#connect-telegram) for how to link it.

In the bot, type:

- **/approvals** to list the courses waiting for your approval, with a link to the approval queue.
- **/summary** (or **/overview**) to get the academy department overview: each active department's head, students, and ongoing and total courses.
- **/help** to see your commands, and **/disconnect** to unlink Telegram.

If you are also a department head, you also get **/pendingfeedback** and **/department**.

## A good routine

- **Every week:** open the dashboard. Look for departments with low numbers or a falling **Last 6 weeks** line, and send a follow-up where needed. Check **Course approvals**.
- **Before each term:** check the survey [statements](./statements-and-options) and [checkpoints](./qualitative-survey-setup). Make sure the survey is **Accepting responses**.
- **At the end of each term:** review and approve closed courses so their records are archived.

## Common questions

**Can I change a department's courses or enrollments?**
No. Department heads manage courses, activities, weeks and students. You can view them. You can manage the **curriculum courses** and **activity types** of any department.

**Do I see other academies?**
No. You only see the academy you signed in to.

**Why can't I choose a department on my profile?**
Your role covers the whole academy, so your account isn't tied to one department. This also means no department head can change or deactivate your account.

**What time zone does APD use?**
East Africa Time. Dates and times on every page, and in notifications, are Ethiopian local time.
