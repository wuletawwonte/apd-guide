# View departments and curriculum

As the quality lead you can open **any department** in your academy. You can look at its courses, students, instructors and results. You can also manage its **curriculum courses** and **activity types**.

::: tip Who can do this
Education quality leads. Deans use the same **Department** menu to view departments.
:::

## What you can do in a department

| Page | What you can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Home** | See the department summary and courses that still need weekly feedback. |
| **Overview** | See performance and survey results for every course. Download them. |
| **Courses** | Open any course and read its activities, weeks and details. |
| **Students** | See the department's students. |
| **Instructors** | See the department's instructors. |
| **Curriculum Courses** | **Add, edit, delete, import and export** curriculum courses and their activities. |
| **Activity Types** | **Add, edit and delete** activity types (for example Lecture, Laboratory). |

Courses, activities, weeks and enrollments are managed by the **department head**. You can view them but not change them.

## Open a department

1. In the left menu, under **Navigation**, click **Department**.
2. The menu changes to show the pages of one department.
3. To choose another department, click the department name at the top of the menu. A list opens.
4. Type part of the name in **Search departments…**, or click a department in the list.
5. APD opens that department's **Home** page.

![The department list open at the top of the menu](/screenshots/quality-lead/department-switcher.png)

To go back to your main menu, click the **back arrow** next to the department name.

![The menu of one department, with the back arrow at the top](/screenshots/quality-lead/sidebar-department-pane.png)

::: tip Faster way from the dashboard
On the **Quality office dashboard**, each department row has a **house** icon (opens **Home**) and a **chart** icon (opens **Analytics**).
:::

## The department home page

The **Home** page shows:

- the number of **instructors**, **students**, **courses** and **curriculum courses**,
- **Courses needing feedback** — courses with past weeks that still have no performance feedback from the head,
- the most recent instructors and students.

![Department home page with counts and courses needing feedback](/screenshots/quality-lead/department-home.png)

## See performance for all courses

1. Open the department and click **Overview**.
2. The **Courses** tab lists every course with:
   - **Qual.** — the student survey score for the course,
   - **Quantitative** — how much of the planned teaching happened. Green is good, yellow is medium, red is low.
3. Use the buttons to filter: **All**, **Ongoing**, **Upcoming**, **Done**.
4. Choose **Session** or **Hour** to measure by class sessions or by hours.
5. Type in the search box to find a course by name or code.
6. Click the **download** icon to save the list as a file.
7. Click a course row to open its full analytics.

![Department analytics with the list of courses, their survey score and progress bars](/screenshots/quality-lead/department-overview.png)

## See student feedback for a course

1. In **Overview**, click a course.
2. Click the **Survey** tab.
3. You see the number of **survey submissions**, the **average qualitative score** and the number of **survey statements**.
4. Click **Qualitative details** to see each statement.

![Course analytics Survey tab with the Qualitative details button](/screenshots/quality-lead/course-survey-analytics.png)

On the **Qualitative details** page, each statement shows its **average score** (out of 100) and a bar chart. The chart shows how many students chose each answer.

![Qualitative details for one statement: average score and answer chart](/screenshots/quality-lead/course-qualitative.png)

::: tip Use this before approving
Look at the survey results when you review a submitted course. See [Approve or return courses](./course-approvals).
:::

## Manage curriculum courses

A **curriculum course** is the official course in the curriculum (for example *Software Engineering Principles, SWE-3101, 3rd year, Semester I, 102 hours*). Department heads create the running courses of each term from these.

1. Open the department and click **Curriculum Courses**.
2. You see the **Curriculum Course catalog** with name, code, year and term, and hours.

![Curriculum course catalog of a department](/screenshots/quality-lead/curriculum-courses.png)

### Add a curriculum course

1. Click **New Curriculum Course**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Code** and (optional) **Description**.
3. Choose the **Year level** and, if it applies, the **Semester**.
4. Type the **Expected hours** for the whole course.
5. Click **Save**.

![New curriculum course form](/screenshots/quality-lead/curriculum-course-form.png)

### Add curriculum activities

A curriculum course is split into **activities**, such as a lecture and a lab, each with its own hours.

1. In the catalog, click the course name (or the **eye** icon).
2. Under **Curriculum activities**, click **New curriculum activity**.
3. Choose the **activity type** and type the **Expected hours**. The form shows the **Remaining Expected Hours** you can still give.
4. Click **Save**.

![A curriculum course with its curriculum activities](/screenshots/quality-lead/curriculum-course-show.png)

### Edit, delete, import or export

- **Edit:** click the **pencil** icon on the row.
- **Delete:** click the red **trash** icon and confirm.
- **Import:** click the **import** icon next to **New Curriculum Course**. Download the example template, fill it in, and upload it.
- **Export:** tick the courses you want, then click the **download** icon.

::: warning Talk to the department head first
Running courses are built from the curriculum. Before you rename or delete a curriculum course, agree the change with the department head.
:::

## Manage activity types

Activity types are the kinds of teaching a department uses, such as **Lecture (LEC)**, **Laboratory (LAB)** or **Tutorial (TUT)**. Each department has its own list.

1. Open the department and click **Activity Types**.
2. To add one, click **New activity type**. Type a **Name** and a short **Code**, then click **Save**.
3. To change one, click the **pencil** icon. To remove one, click the red **trash** icon.

![Activity types list of a department](/screenshots/quality-lead/activity-types.png)

## Common questions

**Why can't I add or edit a course?**
Running courses belong to the department head. Ask the head to make the change.

**Why do I see only one department at a time?**
Each department's pages are separate. Use the department list at the top of the menu to switch.
