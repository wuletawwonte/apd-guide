# Close and submit a course for approval

When a course is finished, you **close** it and send it to the **quality office** (the education quality lead) for approval. The quality office checks it and either **approves** it or **returns** it to you with a reason. An approved course is archived and can no longer change.

::: tip Who can do this
Department heads submit. Education quality leads approve or return.
:::

## How the approval process works

1. **You run the course.** Every week, you give performance feedback.
2. **You close and submit.** When the course has ended and has at least one activity with delivered weeks, you click **Close & submit**. Past weeks that still have no feedback go to the quality office as **not reported**.
3. **The quality office reviews.** The quality leads get a notification. The course shows **Submitted for approval**, and it is locked until they decide (see below).
4. **They decide:**
   - **Approve** → the course becomes **Archived**. It is read-only for everyone.
   - **Return** → the course is open again, with their reason in a yellow banner. You fix the problem and submit again.

You get a notification (the bell at the top) when the quality office approves or returns your course.

## Check that the course is ready

Open the course. Next to the **Close & submit** button you see a counter such as **1 of 2 weeks reviewed**.

- If the course is still running, the button is grey and a note says **Not ready to close yet. The course runs until …**. **Close & submit** unlocks once the course has ended.
- If the course has no activity with delivered weeks, the button is grey and a note says **Nothing to submit yet**: there is no performance for the quality office to review. Point at the grey button to see the reason.

- If the course has ended but some past weeks still have no feedback, a note says **Some weeks have no feedback.** It tells you how many. **Close & submit** still works, but the quality office sees those weeks as **not reported**.

![A course that is still running: a grey Close & submit button and the Not ready to close yet note](/screenshots/department-heads/c-course-not-ready.png)

- Give feedback on the missing weeks first if you can. See [Weekly performance feedback](./performance-feedback). You can still do this after the course has ended.
- When every past week has feedback, the counter turns green with a tick.

![A ready course: 2 of 2 weeks reviewed and an active Close & submit button](/screenshots/department-heads/c-course-ready-to-submit.png)

::: tip Use your dashboard
**My dashboard** has a card called **Ready to close and submit**. It lists your courses that have ended. A course with missing feedback shows how many weeks are **not reported**, in orange.
:::

## Submit the course

1. Open the course.
2. Click **Close & submit**.
3. APD asks: *Close this course and submit its performance to the quality office for approval?* If some past weeks have no feedback, the question starts with how many, for example *2 past weeks have no performance feedback and will be submitted as not reported.* Click **Close**.
4. A green message says **Course submitted to the quality office for approval.**

The course now shows a blue banner **Submitted for approval** with the date you submitted it.

![The blue Submitted for approval banner on a course](/screenshots/department-heads/c-course-submitted.png)

::: info While the course waits for review
After you submit, the **Close & submit** button disappears until the quality office decides. While the course is submitted it is locked, so the quality office reviews exactly what you sent:

- You cannot edit or delete it or change its hours.
- You cannot add, edit or delete its activities, or change their instructors.
- You cannot add, remove, void or restore weeks, or give or change weekly feedback.
- You cannot enroll or remove students or informants.

If something needs fixing, ask the quality office to return the course to you.
:::

When the quality office opens your course to approve it, APD checks the rules again. If the course no longer has any delivered weeks, they can't approve it and will return it to you instead.

## If the quality office returns your course

The course opens again and shows a yellow banner **Returned by the quality office** with their reason. It also appears on **My dashboard** under **Returned by the quality office**.

![A returned course with the reason shown in a yellow banner](/screenshots/department-heads/c-course-sent-back.png)

1. Read the reason carefully.
2. Fix what they asked for. In the example above, weeks 6 and 7 need feedback.
3. When the course is ready again, click **Close & submit** again.

## When the course is approved

The course shows a green **Archived** banner with the name of the approver and the date. The **Edit**, **Delete** and **Close & submit** buttons are gone.

![An archived course with the green banner](/screenshots/department-heads/c-course-archived.png)

An archived course is frozen:

- Nobody can edit or delete it.
- Its weeks, plan and feedback cannot change.
- You cannot add activities, change hours, enroll students or change instructors.
- You can still open it and read all its data and analytics.

## Common questions

**Why is Close & submit grey?**
The course has not ended yet, or it has no activity with delivered weeks. The note under the header tells you which. Missing weekly feedback does not stop you from submitting.

**I don't see Close & submit at all.**
The course is already submitted or archived, or you are not the head of this department.

**Can I take back a submission?**
No. Ask the quality office to return the course to you.
