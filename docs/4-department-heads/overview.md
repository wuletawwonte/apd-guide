# Department heads — start here

As a **department head**, you run your department in APD. You set up the courses for each term, add students and instructors, and give weekly feedback on how each class is going. When a course ends, you send it to the quality office for approval.

This page explains your two main starting screens: **My dashboard** and the department **Home** page.

::: tip Who can do this
Staff members who are the head of a department. Your academy admin or quality office gives you this role. If you are not a head yet, you can ask for it — see [Request department head access](../3-instructors/request-department-head-access).
:::

## What you do in APD

| Task | Where to go | Page in this guide |
|------|-------------|--------------------|
| See what needs your attention | **My dashboard** | This page |
| Look at your department's numbers | **Home** and **Overview** | This page and [Department overview (analytics)](./department-overview) |
| Set up the kinds of sessions you run (lecture, lab …) | **Activity Types** | [Activity types](./activity-types) |
| Keep the course catalogue | **Curriculum Courses** | [Curriculum courses](./curriculum-courses) |
| Open a course for this term | **Courses** | [Courses](./courses) |
| Add activities and weeks to a course | **Courses** → a course | [Activities](./activities), [Activity weeks](./activity-weeks) |
| Add students and put them in courses | **Students** | [Students and enrollments](./students-and-enrollments) |
| Add instructors and give them activities | **Instructors** | [Instructors](./instructors) |
| Give weekly feedback | **My dashboard** or a course | [Performance feedback](./performance-feedback) |
| Send a finished course for approval | A course | [Submit a course for approval](./submit-for-approval) |

::: info Set things up in this order
Each step needs the one before it:

1. **Activity types** (for example Lecture, Laboratory).
2. **Curriculum courses**, with their **curriculum activities**.
3. **Courses** for this term.
4. **Activities** on each course. APD creates the weeks for you.
5. **Students** and **instructors**.
:::

## Your menu

After you sign in, the menu on the left has two parts.

- **The department part** (top). It starts with the department name. Below it are **Home**, **Overview**, **Courses**, **Students**, **Instructors**, **Curriculum Courses** and **Activity Types**.
- **Extras** (bottom). Here you find **My dashboard**, **Qualitative Analysis**, **Feedback** and **Release Notes**.

A small shield icon next to a department name means **you lead this department**.

## My dashboard — "What needs you"

**My dashboard** is the first page you see after you sign in. It lists only the work that is still waiting for you. Each row is a link. Click it to go straight to the screen where you do the work.

1. Click **My dashboard** in the **Extras** part of the menu.
2. Read the four cards. The number in the coloured badge shows how many items each card has.
3. Click a row to open it.

![My dashboard with four cards: weeks awaiting feedback, returned by the quality office, ready to close and submit, and activities with no instructor](/screenshots/department-heads/p-my-dashboard.png)

| Card | What it means | What to do |
|------|---------------|------------|
| **Weeks awaiting your feedback** | Past weeks that have no performance feedback yet. It shows how many weeks, and since when. | Click the row. It opens the activity's weeks. Give feedback for each week. See [Performance feedback](./performance-feedback). |
| **Returned by the quality office** | Courses the quality office sent back to you. The reason is shown under the course name. | Click the row, fix the problem, then submit again. See [Submit a course for approval](./submit-for-approval). |
| **Ready to close and submit** | Courses that have ended and have feedback for every week. | Click the row and submit the course for approval. |
| **Activities with no instructor** | Activities on running courses that have nobody assigned to teach them. | Click the row and assign an instructor. See [Instructors](./instructors). |

::: tip A course cannot be closed while weeks are missing feedback
Clear the **Weeks awaiting your feedback** card first. Only then will the course move to **Ready to close and submit**.
:::

If a card says **All caught up**, **Nothing returned**, **Nothing ready yet** or **Every activity is staffed**, there is nothing to do there.

The buttons at the top right (for example **Software Engineering**) open the Home page of each department you lead.

## The department Home page

The **Home** page gives a quick summary of one department.

1. In the menu, click **Home** (under your department name).
2. Use the buttons under the department name to jump to **Overview**, **Courses**, **Students**, **Instructors** or **Curriculum**.

![Department Home page with shortcut buttons, four number cards, courses needing feedback, recent instructors and recent students](/screenshots/department-heads/p-department-home.png)

The Home page shows:

- **Four number cards**: **Instructors**, **Students**, **Courses** and **Curriculum Courses**. Click a card to open that list.
- **Courses needing feedback**: courses that still have past weeks without feedback. It tells you how many activities and weeks are waiting. Click a course to open its analytics.
- **Recent instructors** and **Recent students**: the newest people added to the department. Click **View all** to see the full list.

::: info "Set up this department" checklist
In a new department, the Home page also shows a checklist called **Set up this department**. It lists the five set-up steps in order (activity types, curriculum courses, curriculum activities, courses, activities). The next step has a button, such as **Add an activity type**. The checklist goes away when every step is done.
:::

## Switch to another department

If you lead more than one department, or you want to look at another department, use the department switcher.

1. Click the department name at the top of the menu.
2. Type in **Search departments…** to find a department, or scroll the list.
3. Click the department you want.

![Department switcher open, showing "Your departments" and "Other departments" marked view only](/screenshots/department-heads/p-department-switcher.png)

- **Your departments** are the ones you lead. You can change anything in them.
- **Other departments** are marked **view only**. You can look at them, but you cannot add or change anything there.

## Common questions

**I do not see "My dashboard".**
You only see it if you have the department head role. Ask your academy admin to check your account.

**My dashboard says "You do not head a department yet".**
You have the role, but no department is linked to you yet. Ask your academy admin to set you as the head of your department.

**Why can I see other departments?**
Heads can look at other departments in the same academy, for comparison. You can only make changes in the departments you lead.
