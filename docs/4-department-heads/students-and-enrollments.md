# Students and enrollments

This page shows you how to find, add and edit students in your department. It also explains the two ways a student is linked to a course, and where to set each one.

::: tip Who can do this
Department heads, for students in the departments they lead. Academy admins can also manage students.
:::

## Two kinds of enrollment

A student can be linked to a course in two different ways. It is important to know the difference.

| Kind | What it does | Where you set it |
|------|--------------|------------------|
| **Course enrollment** | The student takes the course. They can answer the course's **qualitative survey**. | Open the course → **Students** tab → **Enroll student**. |
| **Informant** (activity assignment) | The student reports each week whether a class of one activity was held, and for how long. Informants are the source of the course's performance numbers. | Open the activity → **Students** tab → **Assign student**. |

A student sees nothing in APD until they have at least one of these. Most departments choose one or two reliable students per activity as informants, and enroll the whole class in the course.

## Find a student

1. In the menu, click **Students**.
2. The list shows each student's name, email and **ID number**, newest first.
3. To search, type a first name or a last name in **Search students…**.
4. Click **Open** to see a student, or **Edit** to change their details.

![Students list with the search box and the New student button marked in red](/screenshots/department-heads/p-students-list.png)

::: tip
The search looks at first and last names only. It does not search by ID number or email. If the list is long, use the page numbers at the bottom.
:::

## Add a new student

1. In the menu, click **Students**.
2. Click **New student** (top right).
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Id number** — the student's official university ID, for example `SWE/0999/16`.
   - **First Name** and **Last Name**.
   - **Email** — the student's own email address.
4. Click **Save**.

![New student form with ID number, first name, last name and email filled in](/screenshots/department-heads/p-student-form.png)

APD creates the account in your department and shows the student's page with the message **Student was successfully created.**

![The new student's page showing name, email, department, student ID, last sign-in, total logins and member since](/screenshots/department-heads/p-student-profile-new.png)

What happens next:

- APD emails the student a **temporary password**. They sign in with their email (or ID number) and this password, then choose their own password.
- The student cannot see any course yet. Enroll them in a course, or make them an informant, as shown below.

::: warning Check the email address
The temporary password goes to the email you type. If the email is wrong, the student cannot sign in. Fix it with **Edit** and ask the student to use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
:::

## See a student's details

Click **Open** next to a student. The page shows:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Student ID** | The university ID number. |
| **Last sign-in** | When the student last signed in. **Never** means they have not signed in yet. |
| **Total logins** | How many times they have signed in. |
| **Member since** | The date the account was created. |

A student who shows **Never** may not have received their email. Check the address with them.

## Edit a student

1. Open the student (click **Open**), or click **Edit** in the list.
2. Change the **Id number**, name or **Email**.
3. Click **Save**.

## Enroll a student in a course

You do this from the course, not from the student's page.

1. In the menu, click **Courses** and open the course.
2. Click the **Students** tab.
3. Click **Enroll student**.
4. Pick one or more students from the list. You can type to search.
5. Click the button to confirm.

The students now appear under **Enrolled students**. For more detail and pictures, see [Courses](./courses).

## Make a student an informant for an activity

You do this from the activity.

1. Open the course, then open the activity (for example the lecture or the lab).
2. Click the **Students** tab. The list is called **Informant students**.
3. Click **Assign student**.
4. Pick the students who will report for this activity, then confirm.

APD sends each student a notification: *"You have been assigned as an informant for …"*. From now on they see this activity on their home page and under **My activities**, and can report class sessions each week. The activity's **Students** tab shows how many sessions each informant has reported and when they last did. For more detail and pictures, see [Activities](./activities).

::: tip Add a student from a course or activity
On a course's or activity's **Students** tab there is also a button to create a new student. It opens the same **New student** form. After you save, go back to the course or activity and enroll or assign them.
:::

## Delete a student

1. Open the student.
2. Click **Delete** (red, at the top right).
3. Confirm.

::: danger Deleting removes the student from APD
Only delete an account that was created by mistake. The student will lose access, and their links to courses and activities are removed. If a student left the university, ask your academy admin what your academy's rule is.
:::

## Common questions

**A student says "I see nothing" after signing in.**
Check that the student is enrolled in the course, or is an informant for an activity. Also check that the course has started — upcoming courses have no open weeks yet.

**A student says they cannot report this week.**
Only informants can report class sessions. Check the activity's **Informant students** list. Also check that the week is open. See [Activity weeks](./activity-weeks).

**Can a student be in more than one department?**
Yes. The student's page shows their main department as a badge under their name.

**I cannot see the "Assigned activities" or "Enrolled courses" sections on a student's page.**
Use the course's and activity's **Students** tabs instead, as described above. They are the reliable way to manage enrollment.
