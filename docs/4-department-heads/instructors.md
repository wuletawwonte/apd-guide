# Instructors

The **Instructors** menu lists the staff in your department. Here you can add a new instructor and choose which activities each instructor teaches.

::: tip Who can do this
Department heads, for the departments they lead. Academy admins can also add staff and give them roles.
:::

## Why assign instructors?

Every activity (for example a lecture or a lab) should have one instructor. When an activity has an instructor:

- The instructor sees the activity under **My Activities** in their account.
- The instructor can read the weekly **performance feedback** you give for that activity.
- The activity disappears from the **Activities with no instructor** card on **My dashboard**.

## Find an instructor

1. In the menu, click **Instructors**.
2. To search, type a first or last name in **Search instructors…**.
3. Click **Open** to see an instructor, or **Edit** to change their details.

![Instructors list with the New user button marked in red](/screenshots/department-heads/p-instructors-list.png)

You will also see yourself in this list, because you are staff in the department.

## Add a new instructor

1. In the menu, click **Instructors**.
2. Click **New user** (top right).
3. Type the **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email**. Use the person's work email.
4. Click **Save**.

![New instructor form with first name, last name and email](/screenshots/department-heads/p-instructor-form.png)

APD creates the account in your department and emails the person a **temporary password**. They sign in with their email and this password, then choose their own password.

::: info Roles are set by the academy admin
A new instructor gets the normal staff role (shown as **Regular user**). If the person should be a department head, dean or quality office member, ask your academy admin.
:::

## Assign activities to an instructor

1. Open the instructor (click **Open** in the list).
2. Under **Assigned activities**, click **Assign activities**.

   ![Instructor page with the Assign activities button marked in red](/screenshots/department-heads/p-instructor-profile-new.png)

3. A box called **Select activities** opens. Activities are grouped by course. Click each activity you want. Type in **Search** to find one quickly.
4. Click **Assign**.

![Select activities box with two activities chosen under one course](/screenshots/department-heads/p-instructor-assign-modal.png)

The activities now appear in the **Assigned activities** table, with their code, type, course and expected hours.

![Instructor page listing two assigned activities, with the remove (minus) button marked in red](/screenshots/department-heads/p-instructor-profile-assigned.png)

::: tip Which activities can I choose?
The list only shows activities that have **no instructor yet**, on courses that have **not ended**. If an activity already has an instructor, remove it from that person first (see below), or change it from the activity page. See [Activities](./activities).
:::

## Remove an activity from an instructor

1. Open the instructor.
2. In the **Assigned activities** table, find the activity.
3. Click the red minus button (**Unassign activity**) at the end of the row.
4. Click **Yes** to confirm.

The activity now has no instructor. It will show on **My dashboard** under **Activities with no instructor** until you assign someone else.

## Edit an instructor

1. Open the instructor, then click **Edit**. Or click **Edit** in the list.
2. Change the name or email.
3. Click **Save**.

## Deactivate an instructor

When someone leaves the department, deactivate their account. APD does not delete staff, because their activities and feedback are part of the department's history.

1. Open the instructor.
2. Click **Deactivate** (red, at the top right).
3. Read the message and click **Deactivate** to confirm.

The person can no longer sign in. They leave the instructor list and the pickers. Their activities keep them as instructor until you give those activities to someone else, so reassign them next (see [Activities](./activities#remove-or-change-the-instructor)).

::: info
- You cannot deactivate your own account.
- A deactivated account is read-only and shows a **Deactivated** badge. Only your academy admin can restore it. See [Staff users](../5-academy-admins/staff-users).
:::

## Common questions

**The instructor did not get the email.**
Check the email address with **Edit**. The person can also click **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page to get a new link.

**I cannot see an activity in the "Select activities" list.**
It already has an instructor, or its course has ended.

**Can one instructor teach activities in several courses?**
Yes. Pick activities from as many courses as you need in the same box.
