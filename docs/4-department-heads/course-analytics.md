# Read course analytics

**Course analytics** show how one course is doing: how much of the planned teaching really happened, week by week, and what students said in the qualitative survey. Use it to spot problems early and to prepare your weekly feedback.

::: tip Who can see this
Department heads, instructors, the quality office and deans can open course analytics.
:::

## Open course analytics

1. In the left menu, click **Overview**.
2. On the **Courses** tab, click a course row. (See [Department overview](./department-overview) for the list, filters and export.)
3. The **Course analytics** page opens with three tabs: **Performance**, **Weekly** and **Survey**.

The top of the page shows the course code, its dates, the total number of session reports since it started, and how many reports came in last week compared with how many were expected.

- **Department analytics** takes you back to the list of courses.
- **Course settings** opens the course's details page.

## Performance tab

This tab shows each activity of the course in one table.

![The Performance tab with one row per activity and a course total](/screenshots/department-heads/c-course-analytics.png)

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Activity** | The activity type and group. Click it to open its [weeks](./activity-weeks). |
| **Timeline** | How much of the activity's time has passed, in percent. |
| **Actual** | Hours (or sessions) reported so far. |
| **Expected** | Hours (or sessions) planned up to now. |
| **Total** | Hours (or sessions) planned for the whole activity. |
| **Last week** | Performance in the last full week. |
| **So far** | Actual compared with expected, as a coloured bar. |

The **Course total** row adds all activities together.

Use **Measure by** to switch between **Session** and **Hour**.

Bar colours: **red** below 50%, **yellow** 50–79%, **green** 80% or more.

::: tip How to read it
Compare **Timeline** with **So far**. If half the time has passed but only a small part of the hours were delivered, the activity is behind. Talk to the instructor early.
:::

## Weekly tab

The **Weekly performance by activity** chart draws one line per activity. Each point is one week's performance, from 0 to 100.

![The Weekly tab showing one line per activity across the weeks](/screenshots/department-heads/c-course-analytics-weekly.png)

- A line that drops to 0 shows a week where no class was reported.
- Use **Performance metric** to switch between **Hour** and **Session**.
- Click a colour in the legend to hide or show that activity.

## Survey tab

The **Survey & qualitative snapshot** shows what students said in the qualitative survey for this course.

![The Survey tab with survey submissions, average qualitative score and number of statements](/screenshots/department-heads/c-course-analytics-survey.png)

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Survey submissions** | How many survey responses students sent for this course. |
| **Avg. qualitative score** | The average score of all answers. |
| **Survey statements** | How many statements (questions) the survey has. |

Click **Qualitative details** to see each statement.

## Qualitative details

This page lists every survey statement with:

- the **Average score** out of 100, and
- a bar chart of how many students chose each answer (for example, from *Strongly disagree* to *Strongly agree*).

A red **Required** badge marks statements that students must answer.

![Course qualitative analytics: a statement with its average score and answer chart](/screenshots/department-heads/c-course-qualitative.png)

Click **Back to survey analytics** to return.

::: info
If students have not answered the survey yet, the page says **No qualitative data yet**. Survey questions and timing are set by the quality office.
:::

## Common questions

**Why is the performance 0%?**
No informant student has reported a session yet. Check the activity's informant students and instructor. See [Activities](./activities).

**Why does Last week look good but So far look bad?**
The course was behind earlier but has improved. **So far** counts every week since the start.
