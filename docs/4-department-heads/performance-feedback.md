# Give weekly performance feedback

After each week ends, you rate how well the activity went that week. This rating is called **performance feedback**. It tells the instructor how they are doing, and it is required before you can close the course.

::: tip Who can do this
Department heads give feedback. The activity's **instructor** can read the feedback for their own activities.
:::

## Find weeks that need feedback

There are two ways:

- **From your dashboard.** Click **My dashboard** in the left menu. The card **Weeks awaiting your feedback** lists each activity with the number of weeks waiting and since when. Click a row to go straight to its weeks.
- **From the activity.** Open the activity's **Weeks** tab. Passed weeks without feedback show a **Give feedback** button.

![The Give feedback button on a passed week](/screenshots/department-heads/c-give-feedback-button.png)

## Give feedback on a week

1. On the **Weeks** tab, click **Give feedback** on the week's row.
2. The **Weekly performance feedback** window opens. It shows the week name and dates.
3. Choose a **Feedback type** from the list (see the table below).

   ![The Feedback type list open, showing the five ratings](/screenshots/department-heads/c-feedback-dialog-options.png)

4. Optional: write a **Comment**. Explain what went well or what should change.
5. Click **Save feedback**.

![The feedback window with a rating and a comment filled in](/screenshots/department-heads/c-feedback-dialog.png)

The rating now appears as a coloured badge in the **Feedback** column. Point at the badge to read the comment.

![The saved rating shown as a yellow Needs improvement badge](/screenshots/department-heads/c-feedback-saved.png)

## The five ratings

| Rating | Badge colour | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Disastrous** | Red | Almost nothing planned happened. |
| **Needs improvement** | Yellow | Clearly below plan. Action is needed. |
| **Acceptable** | Blue | Close to plan, with small gaps. |
| **Good** | Green | The plan was met. |
| **Excellent** | Purple | The plan was met or beaten, with high quality. |

Look at the **Performance** bar of the week before you choose. It compares the reported hours or sessions with the plan.

::: warning Check before you save
You cannot change or delete feedback after you save it. Read your rating and comment again before clicking **Save feedback**.
:::

## Rules to remember

- You can only give feedback on a week that has **passed** (red dot). The current and future weeks show a dash.
- Removed weeks (grey, crossed out) do not need feedback.
- Weeks with no feedback show **Pending** to people who cannot give feedback.
- Feedback cannot be added to a course that is **archived**.
- Every passed, non-removed week of every activity needs feedback before you can [close and submit the course](./submit-for-approval).

## Common questions

**Why don't I see a Feedback column?**
You are not the department head for this department, and you are not the activity's instructor.

**I gave the wrong rating. What can I do?**
Feedback cannot be edited in APD. Ask your academy administrator or the quality office how your academy handles corrections.
