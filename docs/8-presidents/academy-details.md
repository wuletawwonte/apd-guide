# Look at one academy

From the Institution dashboard you can open any academy and see how each of its departments is doing.

## How to open an academy

1. Go to the **Institution dashboard**.
2. In the academy table, click the **academy name**.

   ![Click an academy name in the table to open it](/screenshots/presidents/open-academy-link.png)

3. APD shows the academy page with one row per department.

![The academy page with one row per department](/screenshots/presidents/academy-details.png)

4. To go back, click **Back to institution dashboard** at the top.

## What each column means

| Column | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Department** | The department name. Click it to open the department's page. |
| **Head** | The department head. A dash (—) means no head is set. |
| **Courses** | All courses in the department. |
| **Ongoing** | Courses running today. |
| **Avg hourly %** | Average teaching progress of the department's ongoing courses (reported hours compared with planned hours). |
| **Last 6 weeks** | The department's weekly delivery over the last six weeks as a small line, with last week's change from the week before. Point at a week for its numbers. Shown on wider screens only. |
| **Avg reported %** | Share of planned class sessions that informants reported, in the department's ongoing courses. Low means the hourly figure rests on incomplete reports. |
| **Avg qualitative %** | Average student survey score of the department's ongoing courses. |
| **Performance (hourly)** | How many of the department's courses are **High** (80% or more), **Med** (50–79%) and **Low** (below 50%). Shown on very wide screens only. |

## What to do with this information

- A department with a low **Avg hourly %**, a falling **Last 6 weeks** line or many **Low** courses needs
  attention. Open the department to see which courses are behind, and
  [ask its head to follow up](#ask-a-department-head-to-follow-up).
- A dash in **Head** means nobody manages that department in APD. The academy administrator should
  assign a head.

## Open a department

1. On the academy page, click a **department name**.
2. APD shows the department's page. It is read-only. The top line shows the academy and the department head.
3. The table lists every course of the department, running courses first.

![The president's department page with its courses and follow-ups](/screenshots/presidents/department-page.png)

| Column | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Course** | The course name, its code and its dates. |
| **Status** | **Ongoing**, **Upcoming** or **Passed**. |
| **Hourly %** | How much of the planned teaching hours so far were delivered. |
| **Last 6 weeks** | The course's weekly delivery over the last six weeks. |
| **Reported %** | How much of the planned teaching informants reported at all. Low means the hourly figure rests on incomplete reports. |
| **Qualitative %** | The average student survey score for the course. |

4. To go back, click **Back to** and the academy name at the top.

You cannot open a course's own pages from here. For more detail on one course, ask the dean or the
department head, or send a follow-up.

## Ask a department head to follow up

You can ask the head of any department in your university to look into a problem. The head sees your
request at the top of their dashboard and answers it with a reply.

1. Open the department (see above).
2. Click **Follow up** at the top right.
3. Under **Course**, leave **The whole department**, or choose one course.
4. In **What should the head look into?**, say what you noticed and what you want to know. For example:
   *"Delivery has dropped for three weeks and only half the sessions are reported. What is happening?"*
5. Click **Send follow-up**.

The department head gets a notification in APD and by email. When they resolve it, you get their reply as a
notification and by email. The **Follow-ups** list at the bottom of the department page shows every
follow-up for that department (yours and those of the academy's dean and quality office), with an **Open**
or **Resolved** badge and the head's reply.

## Common questions

**Can I see another university's academies?**
No. You only see the academies of your own university.

**Can I download this table?**
Yes. The **Excel workbook**, **PDF** and **CSV: departments** downloads include every department of every
academy. See [Reports and weekly email](./reports-and-weekly-email).
