# Look at one academy

From the Institution dashboard you can open any academy and see how each of its departments is doing.

## How to open an academy

1. Go to the **Institution dashboard**.
2. In the academy table, click the **academy name**.

   ![Click an academy name in the table to open it](/screenshots/presidents/open-academy-link.png)

3. APD shows the academy page with one row per department.

![The academy page with one row per department](/screenshots/presidents/academy-details.png)

4. To go back, click **Back to institution dashboard** at the top.

## What each column means

| Column | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Department** | The department name. |
| **Head** | The department head. A dash (—) means no head is set. |
| **Courses** | All courses in the department. |
| **Ongoing** | Courses running today. |
| **Avg hourly %** | Average teaching progress of the department's ongoing courses (reported hours compared with planned hours). |
| **Avg qualitative %** | Average student survey score of the department's ongoing courses. |
| **Performance (hourly)** | How many of the department's courses are **High** (80% or more), **Med** (50–79%) and **Low** (below 50%). |

## What to do with this information

- A department with a low **Avg hourly %** or many **Low** courses needs follow-up. Talk to the
  **dean** of the academy. The dean can open the department and see each course in detail.
- A dash in **Head** means nobody manages that department in APD. The academy administrator should
  assign a head.

::: tip
The academy page is a summary only. You cannot open a department or a course from here. For course-level
details, ask the dean or the department head.
:::

## Common questions

**Can I see another university's academies?**
No. You only see the academies of your own university.

**Can I download this table?**
Yes. The **Excel workbook**, **PDF** and **CSV: departments** downloads include every department of every
academy. See [Reports and weekly email](./reports-and-weekly-email).
