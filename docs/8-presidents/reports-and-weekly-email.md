# Reports and the weekly email

You can download the Institution dashboard figures as a file, to print or share them. APD can also email
you a short summary every Monday morning.

## How to download a report

1. Go to the **Institution dashboard**.
2. Click **Export** at the top right.
3. Choose a format:

   ![The Export menu with Excel workbook, PDF, CSV: academies and CSV: departments](/screenshots/presidents/export-menu.png)

   | Choose | You get | Good for |
   |--------|---------|----------|
   | **Excel workbook** (XLSX) | One file with two sheets: academies and departments. | Working with the numbers in Excel. |
   | **PDF** | A printable page with both tables. | Printing, or sending to people by email. |
   | **CSV: academies** | One row per academy, plus a totals row. | Importing into other tools. |
   | **CSV: departments** | One row per department of every academy. | Importing into other tools. |

4. Your browser downloads the file. The file name has your university name and today's date, for example
   `arba-minch-university-performance-2026-09-27.pdf`.

The report shows **today's** figures, the same as the dashboard.

### What is in the report

The report has two tables:

- **Academies** — for each academy: departments, courses, ongoing courses, hourly %, qualitative %,
  the number of High / Medium / Low courses, staff, staff active % and students. The last row,
  **All academies**, shows the totals for the whole university.
- **Departments** — for each department of each academy: head, courses, ongoing courses, hourly %,
  qualitative % and High / Medium / Low counts.

![The first page of the PDF report](/screenshots/presidents/pdf-report.png)

## The weekly email report

Every **Monday at 8:00 in the morning**, APD sends you an email with this week's figures for every
academy. The email is **on** by default.

![A sample weekly email report](/screenshots/presidents/weekly-email-sample.png)

The email shows the number of academies, ongoing courses, the average hourly and average qualitative
scores, and one row per academy. Click an academy name in the email to open its page in APD.

### How to turn the weekly email off or on

1. Go to the **Institution dashboard**.
2. Click the **⋯** (More options) button, to the right of **Export**.
3. Under **Preferences**, click the **Weekly email report** switch.

   When the switch is **on**, it shows the day, time and the email address it sends to:

   ![Weekly email report switched on](/screenshots/presidents/weekly-email-on.png)

   When the switch is **off**, it says you won't get the Monday email:

   ![Weekly email report switched off](/screenshots/presidents/weekly-email-off.png)

4. The change saves at once and you see **Saved.** in green. There is no separate save button.

::: tip
The email goes to the email address of your APD account. You can see it in your account menu and in
**My Profile**.
:::

## Common questions

**I did not get the Monday email.**
Check that the switch is on. Look in your spam or junk folder. The email is not sent if your university
has no active academies.

**Can other people get the weekly email?**
The weekly email is only for the president. You can forward it, or download the PDF and share it.

**Do the report numbers change after I download them?**
No. The file keeps the numbers from the moment you downloaded it. The dashboard always shows the latest
numbers.
